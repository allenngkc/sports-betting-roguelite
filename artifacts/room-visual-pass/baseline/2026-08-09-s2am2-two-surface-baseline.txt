  THE DENOMINATOR IS NOT SAFE, and this is the characterization's real result.
    stroke under blur: 2.88 -> 3.63 -> 4.80 -> 4.92 -> 5.68 -> 21.34   (monotonic rising)
    ratio  under blur: 0.774 -> 0.686 -> 0.583 -> 0.653 -> 0.677 -> 0.229   (NOT MONOTONIC)
    The ramp rises with the kernel exactly as predicted. The stroke rises
    FASTER over part of the range, because blur merges adjacent glyphs and
    the falling 50% crossing that ends a stroke runs on into the next gap.
    So ramp/stroke FALLS while the surface gets softer, then collapses once
    the glyphs join. **A larger ratio does not mean a softer surface, and a
    smaller one does not mean a sharper surface, on dense text.** Report the
    RAMP for a regression; the ratio is only meaningful against an identical
    string at an identical size, which is the comparison S2-am2 actually asks
    for -- same surface, same view, over time.
