# 07 — Business & Roadmap

## Market snapshot (researched July 2026 — refresh before launch decisions)

- Steam 2025: ~21,700 releases; median indie grossed ~$249; ~8.5% of new releases hit $100K; indies = 25% of Steam revenue.
- Genre comps: Balatro 5M+ units (solo, 2.5 yrs); CloverPit 1M in <2 months (small team + Future Friends); Raccoin 100K in 24h (Mar 2026); Scritchy Scratchy ~$7, 95% positive.
- Wishlist science (Zukowski): ≥2,000 wishlists before Next Fest or the fest underdelivers (sub-1,000 entrants gained median 462); pre-fest count predicts fest outcome (ρ≈0.825); aim 7–10K at launch for Popular Upcoming.
- **Direct competitor:** *Parlay* (Urple) — unreleased as of Jul 2026. Action item: check its launch performance when it ships; it is free market research on our exact niche.
- Genre risk: third-wave fatigue is visible in reviews; gambling-theme ratings scrutiny (Balatro got PEGI 18). Our satire stance and the sweat/information differentiation are the hedges.

## Pricing & platform plan

$8–13 launch price band (genre-proven). Steam first. Mobile port = v2 revenue wave (Balatro mobile did $9M+ IAP) — protected by the headless-core architecture. Publisher (e.g., Future Friends) considered only if the demo already has organic traction; default is self-publish.

## Marketing channel

Streamers + clips, $0 ad spend. The full-payout stat card and the final-leg sweat are designed as clippable moments. Build the list of ~30 streamers who covered Balatro/CloverPit/Raccoin before launch (action item, Phase 3). Devlog posts on the drama-generator tech may earn dev-community reach (Allen's angle: the math/systems content is genuinely interesting).

## Phases & gates (each gate is a kill/continue decision — killing cheap is the plan working)

**Phase 0 — Design space (now).** Docs 00–06 converged; DECISIONS.md has the core loop locked; `/sim` questions defined.
*Gate:* we both believe the loop on paper; math has no fatal flaw.

**Phase 1 — Headless prototype (4–6 wks part-time).** `/engine` + `/sim`: one sport, moneyline + parlays, ~10 relics, drama generator v0, Monte Carlo survival curves. Playable as ugly console/debug-UI text.
*Gate:* the sweat is tense *even in text*. (If a text parlay sweat isn't gripping, juice won't save it.)

**Phase 2 — Juiced vertical slice (6–10 wks).** Unity layer, minimum viable juice, 1 full run loop, itch.io WebGL build.
*Gate:* strangers replay it unprompted; qualitative feedback names the sweat as the hook.

**Phase 3 — Steam ramp.** Steam page live ASAP after Phase 2 gate (wishlists accrue for months), demo build, 2 more sports, 40+ relics, gurus + insider events in.
*Gate:* 2,000+ wishlists before entering Next Fest.

**Phase 4 — Next Fest + launch.** Fest, streamer keys, launch at 7K+ wishlists. Post-launch: patch cadence, then mobile/v2 (prediction markets) decision based on numbers.

## Success thresholds (from 00-vision)

Strong first-game outcome: $15K net / 500 reviews. Break-glass great: $100K+. Plan finances assuming ~$0; co-op terms are the funding model.
