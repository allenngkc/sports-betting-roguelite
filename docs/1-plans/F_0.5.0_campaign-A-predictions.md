directions, and the sharp simultaneously gained the draw as an instrument. The n=400 smoke hints

be the eleventh entry in the ledger. P2 brackets it wide on purpose.

recorded at the call site). With no items they are never boosted onto it, so I expect little

changed yardstick, and if P11 misses, that is the reason to look at first.
