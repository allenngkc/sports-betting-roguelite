## The finding: G5, and what it is NOT

**Two different things, and conflating them would misread the result.**

- **The synergy is real and strongly measured.** The exemplar reads **+1.22pp at 5.4× its own
  error** — the combo table classes it *superadditive*, not marginal. The composition pillar is
  intact.
- **What cannot be adjudicated is the THRESHOLD's placement.** The 1.0pp floor now sits 0.22pp below
  the reading, and the resolution is ±0.23pp. The gate cannot separate "clears the floor" from
  "sits on it".

**What changed:** the exemplar pair fell **+2.96pp → +1.22pp** under draws. That is a −1.74pp move
against a ±0.23pp instrument, so it is a real compression, not noise. The mechanism is unmeasured
and is not guessed at here.

**Escalation does not fix this, and that is computed rather than assumed.** At 18,500 the resolution
scales to ≈**±0.17pp**; the clearance stays ~0.22pp, giving ~**1.3×** — still below the 2× tier at
which a gate can reliably fail. **A 58-minute escalation would buy no verdict.** This is G3's 2026-08-08
lesson arriving at a different gate: no sample size fixes a gap that small.
