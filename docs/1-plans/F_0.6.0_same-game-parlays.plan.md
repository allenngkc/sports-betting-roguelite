## The invariant that de-risks everything

> **A ticket with at most one leg per matchup must price, pay, void and settle bit-identically to
> today.**

Legs on *different* matchups are independent, so grouping legs by matchup and multiplying the groups'
joint probabilities reduces exactly to today's arithmetic when every group is a singleton. That makes
this change additive rather than a rewrite of ticket pricing, and it protects every existing test and
every gate baseline.

It is enforced two ways, not one:
- **Structurally** — the existing product-of-leg-odds code path is kept *verbatim* and still runs for
  any ticket with no multi-leg matchup group. The joint path is entered only when some matchup
  carries 2+ legs. Recomputing the same number a different way would risk last-bit drift against
  `GoldenSeedTests`; not recomputing it cannot.
- **By test** — a dedicated equivalence test over generated tickets asserting the two paths agree.

## Ticket probability and price, as built

Group legs by matchup. Matchups are independent:

```
p_ticket = Π over matchups m of  p_joint(legs on m)
o_ticket = 1 / (p_ticket × κ × (1 + Ω)^n)          n = total legs, κ = SGP margin dial, default 1.0
```

`Ticket` gains a **locked ticket price**. `PotentialPayout` reads that instead of re-multiplying leg
odds. A leg-targeted Profit Boost multiplies the *ticket* price by the same factor it would have
multiplied the product by — relic value is preserved exactly.

## Phases

Each phase ends: build green, `dotnet test engine.tests` green at or above baseline, Unity DLL
restored. Phases 1–3 are step 3.

### Phase 1 — `engine/JointModel.cs` (new file)
Exact joint probability over a matchup's legs, plus the S73 relation labels. Returns
`(p_joint, relations[])`, never a bare scalar.
- Family split GOAL / CORNER / CARD; joint factorizes across families.
- Goal family summed over the model's **outcome partition `W`**, never a hard-coded two-branch
  winner split — this is what makes it survive draws.
- Scorer term by inclusion–exclusion with the **exact `g < k → 0` guard** (it cancels to ~1e-17
  otherwise and hides impossible tickets).
- Relation classification: `MutuallyExclusive`, `Implies`, `SharedScoreline`, `ScorerOfSide`,
  `Independent`.
- **Exit gate:** single-selection joint equals `MatchModel.TrueProbability` to <1e-12 across a swept
  population, and the relation classifier is total over the shipped board (no combination resolves
  to "correlated but unlabelable").

### Phase 2 — pricing and the guard (`Domain.cs`, `Run.cs`)
- `Ticket` carries the locked price; `PotentialPayout` reads it.
- `PlaceTicket` groups legs by matchup, prices via Phase 1 when a group is non-singleton.
- **Replace** the one-pick-per-matchup guard at `Run.cs:192-193` with the `p_joint = 0` validity
  rejection. Replace, not delete — a zero-probability ticket must still be refused.
- Vig accounting: `fair = 1/p_ticket`, `offered = o_ticket`; `VigPaid` unchanged.
- **Exit gate:** the equivalence invariant above holds; impossible combinations are rejected; a
  known correlated shape prices at the hand-computed joint.

### Phase 3 — void re-pricing
Void-replacement prices computed and **locked at ticket lock**, one per single-void scenario, so
settlement never re-derives distributions. `ActiveLegs`-based payout is retired for same-game tickets.
- **Exit gate:** voiding a leg on a same-game ticket re-prices on the survivors' joint; voiding on an
  ordinary ticket behaves bit-identically to today.

### Step-4 coverage ruling (Allen, 2026-08-12)

The coverage strategy **holds same-match tickets to settlement and does not cash them out.**

Why this needed a ruling rather than a default: cash-out is a live strategy hook (`ShouldCashOut`)
and the campaign tracks cash-out volume, so teaching a strategy to build same-match tickets while
phase 4 is still deferred would have fed a known-wrong price into the very run economy the gates
exist to measure. Holding to settlement keeps every gate number attributable, at the cost of one
explicitly documented gap — same-match cash-out is unexercised in the first campaign, and phase 4
closes it with a re-run.

### Phase 4 — conditional cash-out — **NOT IN STEP 3, lands after step 4's gates (approved)**
`design/02-betting-math.md` now specifies cash-out as
`payout × P(unsettled legs hit | revealed match state)`. This is the most expensive item the model
implies: it needs the enumeration restricted to outcomes consistent with revealed in-match state,
which is materially harder than pre-match pricing. Recommend it lands as its own phase **after**
step 4's gates, so gate re-validation is not blocked behind the hardest code in the lane. Flagged,
not silently dropped.

## Verified before building (step-4 scope, established 2026-08-12)

- **No test and no caller asserts the guard.** Nothing in `engine.tests/`, `sim/`, or the Unity
  scripts depends on "two legs on the same matchup" throwing. Lifting it breaks no existing test.
- **`SkilledStrategy` cannot produce a same-match ticket, by construction.** It builds candidates
  `foreach (Matchup m in slate)` and adds exactly one best candidate per matchup
  (`SkilledStrategy.cs:150-197`), then parlays the top-N of that list. Two consequences, both
  material to step 4:
  1. **Good:** lifting the guard cannot silently move the gate campaign. No strategy changes
     behaviour, so the baseline is stable and any gate movement is attributable.
  2. **Blocking for coverage:** the market-coverage gate (G7) is **blind to this feature** until a
     strategy is deliberately taught to build a same-match ticket. Shipping step 3 without that
     leaves the gates validating a feature they never exercise. This is step-4 work, named here so
     it is not discovered late.
- **Canon names and their authoring boundary** (Allen, batch 48, e729235): the instrument is
  **SAME MATCH** (uppercase market vocabulary, untracked); the mark is **THE HOUSE'S LINE**. The mark
  is *drawn, not captioned* — the name is never printed beside the oxide line, and lives only in rules
  copy, the ledger, and first-encounter.
- **Phase 1 gains a `principal` output** (from batch 48's "states the relation, once per slip"). A
  four-leg same-match ticket carries up to six pairwise relations; the slip states one, so the model
  must nominate it — ranked by `|ln ρ_pair|`, ties by precedence. This is an addition to the output
  contract, not a naming detail, and it is recorded in `design/02-betting-math.md`.
  Both this and the name constants land as a follow-on edit when Phase 1 reports, deliberately not
  injected into the in-flight math dispatch: `principal` is a ranking over relations the evaluator
  will already have computed, so it is additive, while interrupting a delicate numerical
  implementation mid-flight is not a trade worth making.

## Step 4 — the gate campaign (design decisions, 2026-08-13)

**Seed protocol.** `HOLDOUT`, `HOLDOUT2` and `HOLDOUT3` are all burned — F_0.2.0 and F_0.4.0 both
validated on HOLDOUT3. This lane takes a fresh namespace, **`HOLDOUT4-`**, and it is spent once.
The control run goes on **`TUNE-`**, which is already burned and therefore costs nothing; running
the control on the holdout would spend it before the result it exists to validate.

**Two runs, so any movement is attributable.**
- **A — control**, `TUNE-`, current code, no probe bot. Confirms step 3 did not move the economy,
  which the bit-identity invariant predicts but has never been checked end-to-end through a full
  campaign.
- **B — validation**, `HOLDOUT4-`, with the same-match arm. Anything that differs from A is
  attributable to same-match tickets rather than to the engine change underneath them.

**The SGP arm is a dedicated probe bot, not a change to the skilled bot.** This follows from the
model's own EV-parity property: at `κ = 1` a same-match ticket is EV-identical to an equivalent
independent parlay, so a rational bot is *indifferent* and will never build one on merit. Teaching
the skilled bot to build them anyway would be injecting arbitrary behaviour into the primary economy
measurement purely to satisfy a gate — gaming the gate, not covering the feature.

`MartyrStrategy` is the established precedent: a dedicated bot that exists to exercise one path for
one gate. The same-match probe follows it, and **holds its tickets to settlement without cashing
out**, per Allen's approved ruling — same-match cash-out is still naive-priced until phase 4, so
cashing out would feed a known-wrong number into the campaign.

**G7's SGP arm.** G7 keys on `MarketKind`, and a same-match ticket is a ticket *shape*, not a market
— so the criterion needs extending, not just a strategy. It must assert that same-match tickets were
placed and settled, **and that zero tickets were sold at the no-label naive-product fallback.** That
second half discharges the obligation S73 created: a silent fallback is a money leak worth up to
+274% EV, and canon requires it counted and surfaced by the campaign rather than logged.

**The probe bot's policy** (`samematch`, modelled on `MartyrStrategy`'s shape — a bot that exists to
exercise one path for one gate, with its reasoning in its own doc comment):

- **Bet:** deliberately place 2+ legs on one matchup, spread across shapes so the relation vocabulary
  is actually exercised rather than one case repeated — a goal-family pair (`SharedScoreline`), a
  count band (`SharedCount`), a nested pair (`Implies`), a scorer beside its team's goals
  (`ScorerOfSide`), and a cross-family pair (`Independent`).
  **Correction, found in build:** `OVER 2.5 + BTTS YES` is *not* a `SharedScoreline` example on
  today's board — with draws unrepresentable, BTTS YES forces at least 2–1 and therefore **entails**
  OVER 2.5, so the model labels it `Implies` and is right to. This is step 1's own draw-artefact
  finding (`BTTS Y ⊂ O2.5`) reappearing in the catalogue, which is a good sign the model and the
  research agree. The probe uses `Moneyline + BTTS` instead, which holds either way; the pair flips
  back to `SharedScoreline` once Lane 1's draws land.
- **Attempt impossible combinations too**, and handle the refusal. The refusal path is live product
  behaviour now, and a campaign that never trips it has not covered it.
- **Never cash out** — Allen's ruling. Same-match cash-out is naive-priced until phase 4, so cashing
  out would feed a known-wrong number into the campaign.
- **Take Mulligans, and shop for them.** This is a deliberate coverage choice: void re-pricing is the
  riskiest path step 3 added and is currently proven only by unit tests. A probe that never voids
  leaves it unexercised end-to-end. The relic confound this introduces does not matter here — the
  probe is not an economy gate (G1–G6 read other bots), it is a coverage instrument.
- **Out of reach at `κ = 1`:** the sub-evens replacement and its refund need `κ ≳ 1.3`, so that path
  stays unit-test-only in this campaign. Stated rather than quietly uncovered.

**What G7's SGP arm asserts** — pass/fail on the structural facts only, mirroring G7's own stated
philosophy that coverage is structural while exposure is a table you read: same-match tickets
**placed > 0 and settled > 0**, and **`NoLabelFallbacks == 0`**. Relation-kind coverage is reported
as an exposure table, informational, not a gate — a relation seen once is covered, but thinly, and
that is a reading rather than a verdict.

### Run A — control result (TUNE, 2026-08-13): ALL 7 GATES PASS

10,000 runs per batch, 9 strategies, seeds pinned to `TUNE-{i}`, step-3 engine change in place, no
probe bot. **The economy did not move.** The bit-identity invariant predicted this; it had never been
checked end-to-end through a full campaign, only in unit tests.

| Gate | Verdict | Actual |
|---|---|---|
| G1 honest gambling | PASS | median 4, won 0.0% |
| G2 engine mandatory | PASS | median 5, won 0.0% |
| G3 skilled + items | PASS | median 6, won 5.4% (0.9pp from the nearest band edge) |
| G4 the EV arc exists | PASS | crosses at R3 |
| G5 composition superadditive | PASS | synergy excess +3.0pp |
| G6 martyr guard | PASS | martyr-worst 5.8% vs skilled 5.4%, margin +0.4pp |
| G7 market coverage | PASS | all shipped markets covered |

G3's 5.4% matches the band note's own record of the economy reading 5.4–5.5%, which is the strongest
available evidence that nothing shifted underneath. **These are the numbers run B is compared
against**: anything that moves is attributable to same-match tickets, not to the engine change.

**Metrics discipline, because the harness runs batches under `Parallel.For`.** `Harness.cs` states
its own rule at the top: the parallel loop only fills a pre-sized `results[i]`, and *every aggregate
is reduced*. So same-match ticket counts are **per-run fields on `RunResult`, reduced afterward** —
not a second process-wide counter. The existing `SameMatchModel.NoLabelFallbacks` is interlocked and
therefore safe, but it is process-wide by design and reads as one campaign-level number; that is the
right shape for a zero-assertion and the wrong shape for per-bot attribution. If it is ever non-zero,
the per-ticket `SameMatchPrice.NaiveFallback` flag is what attributes it.

**κ stays at its shipped 1.0 for run B.** If the gates hold there, `κ = 1` is validated and needs no
tuning; EV parity says they should. If they move, κ becomes the lever — and note that moving it is
also what makes the plural-remedy and sub-evens-replacement paths live.

## Risks

- **Cross-worktree collision with markets-pregame (Lane 1), which is introducing draws now.**
  Mitigated by construction, not by luck: the joint evaluator goes in a **new file**, and this plan
  targets **zero edits to `MatchModel.cs`**. Residual overlap is `Domain.cs` (they may extend
  `MarketSelection`/`MatchStatLine`; I add a Ticket field — different regions) and `Run.cs` (they are
  unlikely to touch `PlaceTicket`). Sequencing is the orchestrator's; this plan assumes nothing about
  their timeline and works either merge order.
- **Golden-seed drift.** Addressed by keeping the existing path verbatim rather than recomputing.
- **`κ` is unvalidated.** Default 1.0 gives EV parity with an equivalent independent parlay; its real
  value is step 4's to find.
- **The no-label fallback is a money leak if it ever fires** (up to +274% EV on an implication pair).
  It must be counted and surfaced, not logged — a step-4 obligation created by S73.
- **Every measured figure in the design section is pre-draws** and needs re-measuring once draws land.
