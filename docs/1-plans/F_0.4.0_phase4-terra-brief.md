## 2. Non-negotiable laws

1. **No golden re-pin.** The Phase 1 golden pins (`engine.tests/GoldenSeedTests.cs`,
   `unity/SBR/Assets/Probe/GoldenReplay.cs`, `Assets/Tests/EditMode/DeterminismEditModeTests.cs`)
   must pass UNTOUCHED at the end of Phase 4. A golden failure here is a bug in your change,
   never a re-pin. Corollary: you may not add ANY draw to the sequential Slate stream or the
   Outcomes stream. The `SlateGeneratorTests` draw-order pin must also pass untouched.
2. **Stream isolation.** All new randomness uses a NEW match-keyed derived stream:
   add `RngHub.DeriveMatch(int round, int matchupIndex, string purpose)` minting the key
   `"{round}#M{matchupIndex}#{purpose}"` with the same FNV-1a keying discipline as the existing
   5-component `Derive(...)` (see `engine/RngHub.cs`).
3. **Pricing and grading share one distribution.** Whatever model prices a market must be the
   same model that grades it. No approximations on one side only.
4. **Locked odds are the contract.** Offers are priced once at slate generation and frozen.
   Guard: any offer pricing at decimal odds <= 1.0 throws at slate build (see the existing
   pattern in `engine/MatchModel.cs` `Offer(...)`).
5. **Engine/client split.** All pricing/grading/attribution logic in `engine/`. Console and
   Unity only render and validate. `Leg.Side`/`Pick.Side` THROW for non-moneyline selections —
   never call them on market legs; use `Selection`.
6. **Cash-out honesty is free — don't break it.** `DramaGenerator` and `SweatSession` read only
   `leg.TrueProb` / `leg.State` / `leg.OfferedOdds`. Do NOT modify either file; implement
   `TrueProb`/grading for the new market and honesty follows.

## 3. What to build

### 3a. Players on teams (engine)

- `Player` on `Team`: name, role (FW/MF/DF — no GK scorer offers), and a scoring **weight**.
- Roster size and role weights are `RunConfig` dials (defaults: 7 listed players per team;
  weights by role so forwards dominate, e.g. FW 3.0 / MF 1.5 / DF 0.5 — normalize per team).
- Rosters generate at slate-generation time from `DeriveMatch(round, matchupIndex, "roster")` —
  **zero draws on the sequential Slate stream** (law 2). Player names: reuse the deterministic
  fictional-name approach used by `SlateGenerator` teams (satire tone; no real people).

### 3b. Scorer attribution (engine)

- At `Run.LockRound`, AFTER the existing stat-line bake, attribute each baked goal to a player:
  `MatchModel.SampleScorers(statLine, rosters, DeriveMatch(round, i, "scorers"))` — one
  categorical draw per goal, weights = the player scoring weights of the SCORING team (home
  goals draw from the home roster, away from away). Store the ordered scorer list on
  `MatchStatLine` (home scorers + away scorers, in goal order).
- Variable draw count is fine INSIDE this isolated stream; the Outcomes stream must not move.

### 3c. Anytime Scorer market (engine)

- `MarketKind.AnytimeScorer` exists; `MarketSelection.PlayerIndex` exists (default −1).
  Replace the two `NotSupportedException` branches in `engine/MatchModel.cs`
  (`TrueProbability`, `Grades`) with real implementations.
- **Pricing (exact, no RNG):** for player i on a team with unconditional goal-count
  distribution P(G=g) (compute from the SAME enumeration spine: mixture of the two
  conditional score enumerations by TrueHomeProb — see `ScoreProbability`), and normalized
  weight w_i within their team:
  `p_i = 1 − Σ_g P(G=g) · (1−w_i)^g`
  This is exactly consistent with per-goal categorical attribution (3b) — pricing and sampling
  share the model (law 3).
- **One-way market:** YES only. `q = p_i · (1 + Overround)`, odds `1/q`, odds floor guard
  (law 4). Add offers for every rostered non-GK player, both teams, to `BuildOffers`.
- **Grading:** selection wins iff the baked scorer list contains that player.
- `DisplayLabel`: `"PLAYERNAME ANYTIME — Away v Home"`.
- NOTE: `MarketPricingTests.Phase_one_board_contains_...` pins 22 offers per matchup. Adding
  scorer offers changes the board size — update that pin deliberately (a test change with a
  comment), and count scorer offers by roster maths, not a magic number.
