SureThing element kit

Every component of the annotated form guide, at real pixel size, in every state, with the rules that govern it. This is the implementation reference — the Unity build should not have to infer anything from the mockup that is not stated here.

01The laws

ONE · Oxide red #B4483A is the house's mark and nothing else. It is never "you lost". Losing is drawn as a strike-through and a drop toward the ground colour.
TWO · Wax amber #D9A441 is money and the primary action. Biro blue #5E86B8 is anything he chose. Nothing else may borrow either.
THREE · Nothing on this screen is pure black. The ground is #16160F, lifted clear so the room's own shadows stay darker than the laptop.
FOUR · Any text stating a product fact is at least 13px. 12px exists only for OS chrome that carries no product meaning. Disabled reasons, state labels and market navigation are never below 13px.
FIVE · Status is never colour alone. Every state carries a mark, a glyph, a border change or a position change as well.

08The loud register

Calm to build, loud to sweat — and "loud" here is the document being marked at him, not the app becoming a slot machine. Motion is continuous and hand-paced: ink laid down, never panels flipping. That is deliberately the opposite of the TV's quantised refresh in the same room, because if the two screens feel the same one of them is wrong.

NOT INHERITED FROM THE TV · No coarse grid, no monumental type, no brightness-as-sole-channel, no quantised un-eased motion. The TV is an instrument you watch; this is a tool you operate, and it owes the hand continuous feedback.

The whole surface renders inside the game's unified post-process grade — grain, haze, bloom, chromatic aberration, vignette — so nothing here may depend on being pixel-clean. The document's own toner grain sits under that pass, not instead of it.
