# Phone — reference frame capture contract

**Seat:** Design Director (`main-2` terminal) · **2026-08-09** · **Not a ruling.** A capture contract:
what the frame must contain for the phone's owning document to be writable against it (C26-am3).

**Why now:** C38 closed, so the phone is clear for the first time. Every previous look at this surface
was through the softness — **this is the first frame on which its treatment can actually be judged.**

---

## 1. What the owning document will have to decide

Shoot against these, not "the phone in general". Five decisions, and the frame exists to make each
one answerable:

1. **Type** — face, sizes, and the surface's own fact floor. Currently unspecified; the build renders
   in something that is not the studio's face.
2. **Voice** — the copy is sentence-case lowercase with terminal full stops, one object away from the
   laptop's uppercase. Two voices in one glance is the finding that carries C26-am3.
3. **Palette** — the `BOOKIE` header's hue (unmeasured, and T9's retired `chromeCyan` is live on
   another surface), the panel ground, the ink.
4. **Composition** — the screen is ~85% empty with its brightest element at the foot. What the surface
   *is* when the bookie has not spoken is a design question, not a leftover.
5. **Its role in the room** — R19(a)/R28 place it in the laptop's register, not the TV housing's. It
   is *his* phone.

## 2. States required

The surface is characterized by its range, not by one message. **Four states, all live:**

- **Empty** — no messages. The state the player sees most.
- **One message** — Allen's 2026-08-09 frame reproduced clean.
- **Many** — as many as the feed will produce. Does it stack, scroll, cap, or drop? This is where
  composition is actually decided and **nobody has seen it.**
- **The longest message the engine can emit.** T69 and TV-12/13 were exactly this defect one surface
  over. I would rather find it on a reference frame than after writing a document against a short one.

**R28-am binds the capture: live engine data only, nothing authored.** If the feed cannot produce a
long message, **that is the finding** — the longest real message is the longest message. Do not compose
one for the shot. A mocked string would make the document's measurements fiction, and the guard exists
because this surface has no owner to catch it.

## 3. Views required

Both, because they answer different questions:

- **The seated room view** — is the phone readable in the room at all, or is it an object that merely
  *reads as a phone*? R39-am already ruled its glow carries no cue, so if it is unreadable seated, the
  surface's job in the room is different in kind and the document says so.
- **The focused view** — Allen's pose. Where treatment is judged.

**State the canvas-px → screen-px ratio for each.** It is the number that converts every size decision
in the document into something the player actually experiences (S2-am2's lesson, applied before the
document exists rather than after).

## 4. Measurements, with their spaces named (C33-am3)

- **Header hue and chroma** — CIELAB on linear authored. This settles the cyan question against the
  retired-hue register; an eye cannot, and R39/R40 set that standard on this exact surface.
- **Panel ground, ink, and the screen's ladder** — the panel ground read as the brightest element on
  the phone. On the laptop the brightest thing is money. Whether that is right here is a ruling I will
  make, but only against numbers.
- **Rec.709 luma, display-encoded, for anything ranked** (C33).

## 5. Reproducibility (C34)

**Pin the flow's seed and assert it before shooting.** The feed is live engine data, so its content is
run-state dependent — an unpinned set is not a set, and content-dependent findings are exactly what
this frame is for.

## 6. Riding along — S2-am2's baseline, both surfaces

The lane will be at a clean player-path rig with the Game-view toggle off. **That is the cheapest
moment this baseline will ever be available**, so take it in the same session:

- **ramp ÷ stroke at the acceptance view, on the player's path, laptop and phone.** One number each.
  It is what a future regression gets measured against; today the studio would have to re-run the whole
  hunt to know whether the surface had got worse.
- **Precondition, not optional (C37, and it was my own Owed·2):** characterize the ratio instrument
  against a **synthetically blurred frame of known kernel** first. A baseline recorded through an
  uncharacterized instrument inherits its bias permanently, and this number is meant to outlive
  everyone who took it.

## 7. What is NOT asked

Bounded deliberately, because an unbounded capture ask is how a lane loses a week:

- **No fix, no tuning, no authoring.** Nothing on this surface is being corrected — it has no
  authority to be corrected against. That is the entire point of the exercise.
- **No emission work.** R39/R39-am are closed and their values are granted; the glow is not in scope.
- **No new instrument** beyond §6's characterization, which is desk work.
- **No phone-local blur work.** C38 is closed.

## 8. Sequencing

**Not an interrupt.** Nothing is blocked on this — I said so when I ruled it and it is still true.

Order: **§6's instrument characterization first** (desk work, no rig), then the capture in the room
lane's **next natural window**. The characterization gates the baseline, not the frames — if the
characterization slips, shoot the phone anyway and let the baseline follow.
