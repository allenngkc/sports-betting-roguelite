## T84-am2 — TV's structural reframe is ACCEPTED, and it is stronger than the sweep this seat asked for

This seat asked for *"every slot whose extent, or whose neighbour's position, was derived against the
pre-migration face"* — which is a **judgement call per slot**, and one that would have been made by
whoever ran it.

**TV found the structural fact that removes the judgement:** the row builder **fixes all widths and
never derives them from content**, so **every reserved box on the surface carries an unstated
fits-assumption, sized against the narrower face.** The sweep now tests **every box against its
longest renderable form**, tree-stamped per the directive.

**That is strictly better and it is adopted as the sweep's definition.** It converts a sample into a
**population** — C18 §4.1 satisfied properly rather than approximately. It also retroactively explains
T84's three founding instances: they are not three unlucky slots, they are **three members of a class
that contains every fixed box on the surface**, and the only reason they surfaced first is that they
were the ones somebody looked at.

**One binding condition on the digit-bearing boxes.** The assets are still wired to the *proportional*
set (T82-c), but **tabular is what will ship, and a tabular set is normalised toward the widest
digit** — so for most strings the tabular worst case is **wider** than the proportional one. A sweep
that computes digit-bearing worst cases against the currently-wired metrics therefore **understates
the requirement, and can pass a box that fails the moment the font is wired** — the one direction of
error that matters.

**Ruled: the sweep computes its digit-bearing longest-renderable forms against the TABULAR metrics.**
This is exact arithmetic, not an estimate: every digit is exactly 1000 units and the change is
cmap-only, so every non-digit advance is untouched. **The screening may be computed; the boxes it
flags take rendered confirmation** — which is where §2.5 actually binds, on the claims rather than on
the search.

## C46 — a fixed box carries an unstated assumption about the face it was sized against

**Law, register-level** (C39 / C42 / C43's standing — not proposed for the constitution).

**Where a layout fixes a box's width rather than deriving it from content, that box carries an
implicit claim: that the longest string it can hold fits, in the face it was sized against. The claim
is never written down, and a change of face, weight, tracking or figure set invalidates every such box
at once — silently, and everywhere, because nothing in the box refers to the face it assumed.**

Two consequences, both exercised:

- **Sweep the population, not the suspects.** After any change to type metrics, the set at risk is
  *every fixed box*, not the ones a reader can think of. T84's three founding instances were found by
  eye in two of nine moments; the structural reading found the class.
- **The test is the longest RENDERABLE form, not the current content.** A box passes because of what
  is in it today and fails on the string nobody captured.

**Founding case:** Phase T — the canon face is wider, and a truncation (`ONE TEAM BLANKED` →
`ONE TEAM`), a collision (`CASH OUT $131OLD E`) and a solo clipping (`MARKET SUSPENDED`) all issued
from the same unstated assumption in three unrelated boxes.

**Retrospective fit, named as retrospective and not counted as founding:** S68 on the laptop — the
SKIP ROUND headroom collapsing 45 px to 7 px when tracking was applied at the wrong value. Same shape,
a fixed box and a metric change, one surface over.

**Relation to the neighbours.** Constitution §3.5 says *a bound is not a layout* — that a cap obliges
the layout depending on it to be re-derived. **C46 is its converse and its harder half: a fixed box IS
a bound, an implicit one, and nobody re-derives what they never noticed they declared.** R30's *a
locked band is not headroom* is the same family and already applies to all three surfaces' fixed
canvases, which is why this sits cross-surface rather than in TV's table.

**Promotes to the constitution on a second independent founding case** — S68 is suggestive and this
seat is not counting a retrospective attribution as one.
