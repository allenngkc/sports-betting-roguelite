## C3 as ruled

DD 2026-07-31, on the TV: **eligibility is not simultaneity.** §3's L4 occupants and §7's ball are
*eligible* for the HDR material; an **explicit one-token invariant** holds that at most one is lit at
a time, arbitrated so a momentary punch preempts a sustained state, at one boost value (1.8).

## Restated for TMP materials on the laptop

> **No migrated text slot on the laptop acquires an HDR or emissive material.** Every TMP slot renders
> on the default SDF material at the same tokens it renders today, so **the laptop contributes zero L4
> tokens before and after the migration**, and C3's count and arbitration are untouched by Phase L.

**Three consequences, each checkable rather than declarative:**

1. **The invariant counts tokens lit, not shaders drawing.** Replacing `UI/Default` with TMP's SDF
   shader creates no L4 occupant. **A TMP material carrying emission, or any boost above 1.0, would**
   — silently, on a surface that has never had one. Enabling one to answer a legibility complaint is
   C10's shape: the lever is the token, not the material.

2. **The laptop's brightest type must not get brighter.** The 31px wax payout figure is this
   surface's ceiling. Before/after captures run **on the same pinned seeds** (C34), so this is a
   measurement and not an assurance: it measures no higher after than before, or the material path
   moved and Phase L owns it.

3. **A laptop that gained an HDR token would corrupt the TV's ladder measurements, not only its
   own.** Both surfaces composite through one grade. That is why C15 named C3 as a precondition
   instead of leaving it to Phase T.

## Why the precondition my own scope stated is now met

My handoff previously said this seat should not migrate before the TV's material path was settled,
or the laptop becomes a second variable in someone else's measurement. **T41, T48, T49 and T58 are
all closed as of batch 14**, so the TV's grade, black point, bloom and gold-flash behaviour are ruled.

**The remaining exposure is Phase T's own TMP work, which is still ahead.** That is why clause 1 is
written as a prohibition rather than an observation: if Phase T needs TMP text on an HDR material for
the TV's L4 occupants, that is a live question for the TV — and the laptop having already declared
zero tokens keeps the two separable.

## What this restatement cannot see (C25)

- **It binds the material, not the rendered result.** SDF edges under bloom may composite differently
  from UGUI's bitmap edges at the same token — that is a *render* difference, not an L4 token, and
  the before/after frames are what will show it. I have not measured it and cannot before the assets
  exist.
- **It covers the laptop only.** `InteractionHud`, `PhoneScreen` and the TV's own text stack stay on
  legacy UGUI after Phase L, so the project runs both stacks between phases. Whether that is
  acceptable for the room's captures is the room's call, not mine.
