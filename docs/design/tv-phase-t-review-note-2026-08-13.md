## What this arc cost, and what it bought

Four batches, two refusals, one grant.

**Five of this seat's own errors were corrected on the record:** a routing conflict that put a closing
condition inside a deferred pass; *unbounded* used twice after ruling the word wrong; an
over-generalised list ruling; an offered remedy that did not exist; and a threshold that conflated two
independent quantities. **A sixth was found in the closing frames themselves** — a clearance floor set
on one side of an edge. **The lane found four of the six.**

**The pre-commitment is what made that safe.** The standard was fixed before the evidence existed, so
when the evidence arrived the only question was whether it met the standard — not what the standard
should be. It cost one refusal that was uncomfortable to write and a second capture window that TV
declined to waste. **The condition that failed was the one that would have been easiest to talk past.**

**And C31 is what makes this grant land rather than drift.** A defect was found on the closing frames.
It is real, it is serious, and it does not withhold a grant whose conditions were named in advance and
met. **A gate that grows every time someone looks again is not a gate** — it is a seat that cannot say
yes. This one says yes, opens the item, and writes down which is which.
