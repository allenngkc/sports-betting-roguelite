## Why this one is written in advance

Batch 27 ruled the removal on a prediction: **frame 0 already photographs the outcome.** It is the
flood-at-alpha-0 state and it measures **6.47:1 accept / 6.58:1 win tally**. The ruling called this
*"a pre-verified fix, needing confirmation rather than discovery."*

That is exactly the condition under which a seat is most likely to read frames generously — the
answer is already believed. So the dispositions are fixed now, while the prediction can still be
wrong at my cost.

## Dispositions — fixed in advance

**1. GRANT** — accept and win-tally both reproduce frame 0 within the instrument's own resolution,
measured in **linear relative luminance**, **composited at the element's own pixels** with nothing
drawn between the ink and the camera (C33-am3; C35-am). Predicted 6.47:1 and 6.58:1. A result inside
**±0.35:1** of each is confirmation. Outside it, the prediction failed — the difference is explained
before any grant, because something moved that the removal was not supposed to move.

**2. REFUSE, no partial credit** — the flood is dimmed, alpha-faded, z-ordered beneath, or made
conditional rather than deleted. C10 / §3.3: an effect that fails on mechanism is removed, never
tuned toward invisibility. This has a named history on this exact element and I will not re-litigate
it on a lower alpha.

**3. REFUSE** — only one sibling measured. Divergence between siblings is the defect that produced
T68 in the first place; T71 rides for that reason and no other.

**4. REFUSE** — §6.1's punctuation did not survive. The brief L4 punch then settle measured working
at **0.688 → 0.586 (frame 24)**, and batch 27 found the flood *redundant with* the punch, not
carrying it. If the punch left with the flood, the removal took something that was doing work.
**This is the failure I consider most likely and least likely to be volunteered** — it degrades
quietly and every contrast number still lands.

**5. VOID — returned unadjudicated, re-run, not a refusal** — the measurement is confounded.
Display-encoded space passed off as linear (this seat's own recorded error, C33-am3); or a control
that brackets only the warm-up on an animated beat (C36 — the live risk here, since the thing being
removed was itself a pulse, so a closing control is what certifies the run, never the opening pair).
Per §2.6 a confounded measurement closes nothing, in either direction.

**6. FINDING, not a grant blocker** — `_dimOverlay` went with the floods. Scope overrun explicitly
named in the ruling; the dim returns.

**7. Rider on any grant** — if a bound moved, the layout depending on it re-derives in the same
commit (§3.5, three instances in one fortnight).

## The null clause

If the lane reports the removal changed nothing measurable, that null is void unless the instrument
could have resolved the change it was looking for (C37 / §4.5). It could: the flood peaked at 0.507
against ink at 0.384. A null on a signal that size means the instrument, not the fix.
