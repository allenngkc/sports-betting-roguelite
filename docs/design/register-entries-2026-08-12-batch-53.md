farthest**; the tolerance interval is **96.0–106.8**; the nearest reading is **3.3° below the floor**.

**That failure is not resolvable and I am not going to pretend it is.** The miss is **3.3°** against a

**But the resolution point is not the ruling, because the proxy is the wrong instrument regardless.**

**C40 governs and this is its converse:** *a rule states the property it protects; a proxy is labelled
as one, so a passing proxy is never read as the property held.* **A FAILING proxy must equally not be

**RULED: the property holds. T65's remaining half CLOSES.**

## V6-am3 — the gate checks a value against ITS OWN family, not against one room-wide band

**A single band across four fixture families was always going to be either too loose to catch anything
or wrong for three of them.** It was both.

**Ruled: V6 checks each value against its own fixture family's bound.** The families are named above,
with their values and their provenance. **A value belonging to no family is not gate-able by a family
bound** — it is ruled at this seat against the property, which is what just happened.

**Standing, promoted from room's line because it is the general form:** **a bound is defended by the
reasoning that groups its members, never by how tightly they happen to cluster.** Clustering is
evidence that a grouping might exist; it is never the grouping's justification. Four batches on this
band, and every wrong turn — the phantom top, the lights-vs-screens carve, the cross-population
comparisons — came from treating proximity as membership.
