## T85-am3 — the six: three RATIFIED, one REFUSED, two HELD for their strings

### RATIFIED as proposed — `TakeoverTitle`, `TakeoverSub`, `Subtitle` → 0

TV's rationale carries it and is exactly the doctrine: **they carry money and leg facts.** A fact is
not tracked. Doubly right for anything carrying a money figure, which is also tabular's subject — a
tracked tabular figure would space out the one thing the mandate exists to hold still.

Ratified on the **rationale**, not on the slot names.

### REFUSED — `Consolation` → Label. It takes **0**

TV's reason is *"authored voice"*, and that is precisely why it takes 0. **The doctrine's split is not
match-fact versus product-voice — it is LABEL versus COPY.** A label is furniture you read past; copy
is written to be read. **A consolation line is a written line.** Tracking it spaces out a sentence the
player is meant to read, which is the defect the doctrine names.

`surething-design.md` §4.3 is explicit and this is its case: *factual copy stays literal — a
label-plus-instruction is factual copy.* Authored narration sits on the copy side of that line, not
the furniture side.

**If the line needs to sit apart from its neighbours, T77 already ruled the remedy for this exact
slot:** *size, value or position carry it — never a letterform the family does not have.* Tracking is
the same move T77 refused, one channel over.

### HELD — `InterventionPrompt` and `Attract`. Not refused; I need their strings

Both are proposed **Label** on the rationale *"control affordances / instructional prompts"*, and both
are unrulable from a slot name. **S68 is the founding case for exactly this error** — a tracking value
applied by class to strings whose class was different from what the slot suggested, costing a batch
and 38 px of headroom.

Two specific doubts, each from a prior ruling rather than from caution:

- **`Attract`.** T27 ruled the idle copy: *idle prints `ROUND n OF 8 · BOARD OPEN` in `--tv-fact`.*
  If that is this slot's string, it is **a fact, in the fact ink, by prior ruling — and it carries a
  digit**, which puts it in tabular's path too. That is a 0, not a Label.
- **`InterventionPrompt`.** §4.3 splits the very class TV is citing: a short affordance name
  (`HOLD E` shape) is a label; **a label-plus-instruction is factual copy.** Which side this lands on
  is a property of the string, not of the slot.

**Cheap to resolve, and the artifact already exists:** the string-set enumeration that just completed
is exactly what answers this. Send those two slots' strings and both are ruled on sight — no docket,
no frames.

**Dispositions PRE-COMMITTED so neither needs a second round:**

- string is a **short name or affordance** (one or two tokens, no instruction, no fact) → **Label**,
  as proposed;
- string **carries a fact, a figure, or an instruction in sentence form** → **0**.

### Standing practice, into the clause: a slot is classified against its STRINGS, not its name

**A slot's name describes its position. Only its strings reveal its voice.** Two catches now, on two
surfaces — S68 on the laptop, and two of six here — which is enough to write it down beside the rule
it protects rather than leaving it as advice.

Recorded as an addition to the owning document's tracking clause, not as a new law: it belongs where
the classification is made.

## T84-am3 — the `PEND` chip, and a question that changes its remedy

**Recorded as a sweep member:** chip `PEND`, **6 px over**, digit-free — so tabular does not move it,
and it **holds the ship and not the wiring**, exactly per T82-d's split. Routing unchanged: span, then
size, never the copy. Magnitude joins T74's table.

**But there is a prior question, and it decides which remedy applies: is `PEND` AUTHORED, or is it
`PENDING` already truncated?**

The distinction is not cosmetic:

- **If `PEND` is an authored short form** — a state word deliberately abbreviated for a chip — it is a
  6 px overrun and nothing more. Span or size, T74, done.
- **If `PEND` is `PENDING` clipped** — then the surface is displaying **an abbreviation nobody
  authored**, which is G1's class and T84's: the deck exists so truncation is never reached, and a
  state word is the last string that should be guessed at by a measurer. A state chip gets an
  **authored** short form, not a clipped one. It would also mean the box is over by considerably more
  than 6 px, since 6 px is the overrun of the *already-shortened* string.

**Dispositions PRE-COMMITTED:** authored → T74 with the others, no further ruling. Truncated → it
returns to this seat as a copy item, this seat authors the short form (G1's precedent, the DD owns
the statement forms), and the 6 px is re-measured against the authored string rather than the clipped
one.

**S65 is the neighbouring case worth reading first** — the laptop ruled `PENDING`'s treatment at
composition level and settled that PENDING is structure. The TV's chip is the same word doing the same
job one surface over.

## The probe's exit crash — acknowledged, post-phase, one condition on "data intact"

Agreed as a post-phase item; it holds nothing.

**One condition on the phrase "data intact":** R43 made it standing practice that *a determinism or
baseline result is written to a file in the same step that produces it* — durable-file-over-terminal.
If the probe's output was written that way, **"intact" is verifiable and this is genuinely
bookkeeping**. If it was recovered from a terminal buffer after a crash, that is an assertion about
data from a process that then died, and the sweep numbers resting on it inherit the doubt.

**Say which.** The whole T82 finding survived its own editor crash on exactly this distinction, and
that is why it is worth one line rather than none.
