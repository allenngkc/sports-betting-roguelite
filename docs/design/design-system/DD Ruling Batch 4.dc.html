T21
Ruled · drop
§8.8 stats panel — the two rows the sim cannot source
Drop both rows. Do not re-source them in the presentation layer and do not respec them into something adjacent that the sim happens to emit. The panel ships with the rows it can fill, at its authored height, and does not reserve dark space for rows that have no source. A dropped row returns only when the sim emits that quantity as a first-class value — if the number already exists under another name, that is re-sourcing and it is allowed; if the panel would have to compute, infer, or approximate it, it is not.
Why
The number never lies. A row the sim cannot source can only be filled by fabrication or by a proxy the player will read as the stated thing — and the stats panel is exactly where a player goes to check a fact. Respec is the tempting option and it is the wrong one: it keeps the slot and changes what fills it, which is how a panel ends up meaning something nobody specified. Name the two rows in the transcription so the register records what was dropped.

T22
Ruled
§8.10 held cash-out preview — the confirm gesture
Hold previews. Release always abandons. Release is never confirm. Bind the preview to a held key on the TV — legs struck and dimmed, never extinguished, figures replaced by the offer, exact revert the instant the key is released. Confirm is a separate deliberate act and it belongs on the laptop: taking the money is a commitment, and commitment is SureThing's, not the TV's. The player has to stop watching to take it.
If the cross-surface binding is out of Phase 3's scope, the bounded fallback is: confirm is a second, different key pressed while the preview is held — never the release, never a timer, never an auto-commit, no countdown of any kind. Print both keys as words in the slot at eyebrow size or above.
Copy, ruled with it: [E] alone is not a label, it is a debug token, and it is on a shipped surface in every frame. The slot reads CASH OUT $183 with HOLD E beneath it in context grey. Where another product would draw a glyph, this one prints the word.
Why
Release-to-commit makes abandoning and committing the same physical motion, and money dies on the difference. Beyond that: the surface split is a hard product law. The TV is an instrument you watch; it reveals. The laptop is a tool you operate; it owns the slip, the stake and the lock. A cash-out taken entirely on the TV quietly makes the TV a second sportsbook. And the friction is not a cost here, it is the point — a game whose thesis is that the bettor watches the app instead of the game should make him look away from the reveal to take the money.

R12·a
Amended · as proposed
Surface detail is gated by grazing light
Approved as the lead wrote it, with the mechanism attached — the mechanism is the whole value of the amendment, because R12 v1 failed precisely by having none. A law without a mechanism cannot be tested against a plan before the bake.
Surface detail is gated by lighting, not texture authoring — and specifically by direct light arriving at a grazing angle. Bounce fills shadow; it does not reveal surface.
Relief is a gradient divided by a mean. Probe lighting is spherical harmonics, smooth at the scale of a cushion, so it raises the mean without raising the gradient and can only move the denominator. Any probe-mediated addition therefore lowers relief on a surface that is already lit. This does not contradict R6, where bounce replaced flat ambient and created gradient across metres of wall where there had been none.
Recorded with it: the amendment retired the slab-albedo lever without spending a bake, because that too is a probe-mediated addition. That is a law paying for itself the day it is written, and it is the argument for writing mechanisms into all of them.

S22
D-01 · ruled
Label vocabulary — the fields win
The engine emits fields; the surface composes the label. A packed string is a presentation artifact and must not be the source of truth. Leg exposes market kind, selection, line, team or player, and price; DisplayLabel becomes a per-surface composer rather than a stored string. Adopt the design system's vocabulary verbatim — MONEYLINE, TOTAL GOALS, BTTS — YES / BTTS — NO, TOTAL CORNERS, TOTAL CARDS, ANYTIME SCORER; offer lines OVER 2.5 GOALS, BOTH TEAMS TO SCORE, VALE ANYTIME.
The role tag has no field in the contract because it should not be a tag. Add role as a first-class field on the scorer offer and print it as a tracked-uppercase word in --toner-3 after the name. The form prints words; brackets are a code smell that reached the page.
Why
The same leg has to render into four different widths — a 324px margin, a printed receipt, a ledger row, and a 242px TV ticket column. One packed string cannot serve all four, and we already have the failure in hand: the TV's statement line overflows its column with exactly this kind of string (T25.3). Fields let each surface decide what it can afford to print. This is also the cheapest possible fix for T25.3.

S23
D-02 · ruled
RIDING — the design system is short a word, not the product
Keep RIDING. Amend the enum. PENDING · RIDING · LIVE · GREEN · DEAD · VOID · CASHED OUT, with RIDING ticket-level only and LIVE leg-level only, and the split written into the contract so it cannot drift back.
Why
"This ticket can still win" and "this leg is being played right now" are two different facts, and the obvious fix — reuse LIVE — would give one word two meanings, which is the exact failure the two-vocabulary split was designed to prevent. RIDING is literal (the stake is still riding), impersonal, and not celebratory, so it passes the voice test the enum exists to enforce. The vocabulary had a hole; the product found it.

Law
Wrong in kind is not fixed by opacity
An effect that is wrong in kind is not corrected by reducing its strength. A lightening-only grain at 5% is a fainter version of the wrong thing, not a subtler version of the right one. When an effect fails on mechanism, turn it off and scope the correct one — do not tune toward invisibility and call it shipped. Promoted from S19; the same test applies to bloom, wear, aberration and every future overlay.
