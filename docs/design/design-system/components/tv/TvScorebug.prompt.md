Match truth at the top of the TV's right region: teams, score, clock, ticket/leg index.

```jsx
<TvScorebug ticket="1/2" leg="2/3" away="Nighthawks" home="Foundry" score={[1,0]} clock="67'" backed="home" />
```

The score is the largest element on the surface and nothing outgrows it, cash-out included. Team hues are muted and used only for identity; season records leave the live scorebug entirely. Figures are tabular so the surface does not twitch on every tick.
