The full-screen ticket interstitial between tickets, and the shell the settlement beats reuse.

```jsx
<TvTicketCard heading="TICKET 2 OF 2" risk="$50" pays="$462"
  legs={["NORTHSIDE ML +135","OVER 2.5 -110","VALE ANYTIME +210"]} />
```

It only appears once the stage and active-leg card have cleared, and nothing from the prior ticket may survive into it. Round settlement may reuse this treatment but must never resemble an active leg or a live cash-out offer.
