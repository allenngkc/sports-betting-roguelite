A relic or consumable offer on the REWARDS shop, as a ruled form entry.

```jsx
<OfferEntry name="Vig Rebate" price="$140" affordable={false} reason="BANK TOO LOW"
  description="Returns 4% of every losing stake to the bank at round settlement." />
```

Affordability is never colour-only: unaffordable pairs a muted BUY with a stamped literal reason. The shop is a ruled list, not a promotional rail — no featured cards, no urgency.
