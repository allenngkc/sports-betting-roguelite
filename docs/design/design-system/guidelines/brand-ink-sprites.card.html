Generated, not hand-drawn, and reproducible: python tools/art/make-biro-rings.py. A given seed always produces the same mark. What makes them read as ballpoint: the path is an ellipse deformed by three low-frequency harmonics, the pen overshoots the start, a second partial pass retraces part of the ring sharing the first pass's shape, ink density ramps in and lifts into a tail, short skips drop out, and crossings accumulate darker.

Tint selection rings --biro and strikes --stamp. Variant is deterministic from the matchup index — never randomised per frame or per canvas rebuild.
