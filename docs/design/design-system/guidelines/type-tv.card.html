Tabular numerals are mandatory and non-negotiable. Scores, clocks, money and counts all change in place; non-tabular figures make the whole surface twitch on every tick. The score is the largest element at all times — nothing outgrows it, cash-out included.

Face ruled 2026-07-31: Encode Sans + Encode Sans Condensed (OFL 1.1). §5 asks for a technical grotesque, condensed where the ticket column is dense and wider for the scoreline — a width-axis brief, and this family carries the deepest free width range. Nine weights make weight a real channel for the L1–L2 tiers.

Chosen by measurement, not taste. Saira Condensed ships no tabular figures — rendering 0–9 with font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums left a 7.08px advance spread — so the strongest candidate on character was disqualified by the one rule that has survived every substrate change. Fira Sans is humanist and warm, and §4 spends the whole warmth budget on gold; Barlow's rounded terminals soften the technical read.

Not the laptop's face. Archivo is a gothic text face for small copy on a paper ground; Encode Sans is an engineered screen face with a width system. Same discipline, different instrument — one hand doing different jobs, which is what T11 requires.
