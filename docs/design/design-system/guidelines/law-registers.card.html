SURETHING · THE LAPTOP · CALM
His own machine. Personal, chosen, cheaper, grubbier, possibly customised. A tool you operate: dense, continuous, hand-paced. Owns the slate, markets, slip, stake, staging, lock, shop and placed tickets.
Motion: laid ink. Continuous, undurated, caused by marking the document.
TV SWEAT · THE PANEL · LOUD
A hardened industrial display installed by an institution, not bought by him — riveted steel, chipped paint, stencilled equipment code, conduit continuous with the room's pipes. An instrument you watch. Owns unrevealed drama: score, clock, probability, outcomes.
Motion: panel refresh. Quantised, discrete, one shared LIVE pulse.

Do not carry the TV's vocabulary onto the laptop — not its coarse grid, monumental type, institutional steel, brightness-only semantics or un-eased motion. Anything on the laptop built from that vocabulary becomes a second TV, and it has failed. MY BETS may only mirror what the TV has already revealed; it never reads engine state or runs ahead of the broadcast.
