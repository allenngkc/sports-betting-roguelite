# Proposal for Allen — replacing the art authority `08` left behind

**From:** Design Director · **Date:** 2026-07-31 · **Decision needed:** shape, then authorship
**Register:** inbox item 2 (studio art-authority gap)

`design/08-art-direction.md` was deprecated 2026-07-24. `tv/DESIGN.md` replaced it **for the TV
only**. Room, laptop and phone have had no binding art authority for a week.

## Do not write one document to replace it

The obvious move is a new studio-wide art direction. That recreates the failure. `08` was one
document asserting one world — casino neon, CRT, green/red/gold — across every surface, and it died
because the surfaces turned out to need genuinely different registers: the laptop is **calm** and
personal, the TV is **loud** and institutional, the room is **painterly** and physical. A law that
was true for one was wrong for the others, and because it was central, being wrong once made the
whole thing an anti-reference. C4 already ratified the principle in miniature — money colour is
**per-surface**, and coherence is a choice rather than a constraint.

## Two tiers instead

**Tier 1 — a studio art constitution.** Two pages. It holds *only* what is genuinely cross-surface and
already ratified, and it adds nothing new:

- the world premise — a rotting bunker in a wealthy high-tech city that has no use for the occupant;
  the machines are nicer than the life;
- the unified grade — one pass over room and screens together, and no surface is exempt from it;
- lifted blacks — nothing in frame is `#000000`, and no screen's blacks beat the room's shadows;
- diegesis — every interface is a real screen on a real object, seen at an angle, with glass and dust;
- fictional leagues, teams and players only;
- per-surface colour sovereignty (C4);
- the register split — the laptop decides, the TV reveals, and if the two screens feel the same one of
  them is wrong;
- precedence: constitution → surface document → implementation notes, and **the latest ratified
  document governs** (the rule C1 just set).

Everything else is delegated. If a rule cannot be stated without naming a surface, it does not belong
in tier 1.

**Tier 2 — one owning art document per surface**, all the same shape as `tv/DESIGN.md`, which is the
one that has actually worked: a direction contract (thesis / own-world / story / first viewport /
form), then colour, type, layout, components, state vocabulary, motion, do's and don'ts, and a
supersession header naming exactly what it deprecates.

## Cost and sequencing

Tier 1 is short and mostly transcription. Ratifying the laptop document is a header change plus a
register line. The room document is the only real writing, roughly one session. None of it blocks
R9/R10, T15 remediation, or S9 — this is structural work that pays off at the next disagreement, not
a gate on anything currently moving. **Fine to schedule behind the pending build items.**

The reason not to defer it indefinitely: right now, if two leads disagree about the room or the
laptop, there is no document that settles it — and the register would record the conflict rather than
resolve it, which is the state `08` was supposed to prevent.
