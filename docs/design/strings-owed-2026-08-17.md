## 1 + 3. THE CASHED-OUT TICKET — authored as ONE job, because separately they restate

`T114` measured the defect: `RISK $87` and `PAYS $1,490` printed above `CASHED OUT $199`, held across
all thirty frames of the moment. **Both footer words are false** — the position is closed and it will
never pay $1,490.

**The banner and the footer must be authored together or they collide.** If the footer states the
return and the banner states the return, that is the same fact one slot apart — `T69`/`T70`'s family.

### The ruling

| slot | riding | **cashed out** | source of the word |
|---|---|---|---|
| footer word + stake | `RISK $87` | **`STAKE $87`** | already in the product — the laptop's margin prints `STAKE $35` |
| footer return | `PAYS $1,490` | **`RETURNED $199`** | **already in the product as a PAIR** — `S38` records `STAKE`/`RETURNED` on the laptop ledger |
| banner | *(absent)* | **`CASHED OUT`** — the amount DROPS | the string already exists; only the number leaves |

**`STAKE` / `RETURNED` is borrowed as a pair, not assembled from two singles.** The laptop already
uses exactly this pairing for exactly this meaning — what went in, what came back. **That is stronger
than coining a match: it is the same object, one surface over.**

**The banner drops its amount** because the money now has two true slots of its own. **The state
belongs in the state slot; the money belongs in the money slots.** That is the surface's own division
of labour (`T92`, `T24`).

**`PAID` was considered and rejected**: it collides at the root with `PAY $60` in the run status bar
on the same screen, and it is a coinage where `RETURNED` is a borrowing.

### ⚠ One measurement the lane owes before this builds

**`_tPays`'s box has never been measured.** The consolidated sweep measured `RiskPays` (249.0px,
widest `STAKE $13,639` at 158.9px, 90.1px spare) — **that is the OTHER component.** `RETURNED $199`
is four characters longer than `PAYS $1,490`, going into a box with no recorded width.

**Do not build this until `_tPays` is measured against `RETURNED ${largest return}`.** Batch 95's
lesson, and it is exactly the shape that has caught this studio three times: a string authored into a
box nobody measured.

## 2. THE FLAVOUR STRIP'S SUFFIX — REMOVED

`(12 in the spell)` is `spec.Count.Value.TotalDelta` — **truthful, checked at source, and it goes
anyway.** Four reasons, and none of them is the overrun:

1. **Opaque.** *Spell* is never explained to the player anywhere on the surface.
2. **It misreads as a running total** when it is a per-event delta.
3. **The fact is already SHOWN** — the count moves in the column in front of him. *Drawn, not

4. **The widest string prints `spell` TWICE**, one clause apart — `T69`/`T70`'s defect inside a single

**The overrun is the fifth reason and deliberately the least of them.** A string should not be cut for

player does not need to know whether a jump arrived as one event or two.

**`T110-am`'s measurement still stands and is not superseded.** Measure the decks **without** the
suffix: if the bases fit, this removal closes the overrun and **no deck is re-authored**; if they do
not, rung-2 forms are still needed. **One number, and it decides real work.**

## 4. THE DECISIVE-BEAT POOL — the theater spec's §3.5

**TV builds the disjointness; the pool is authored here.**

### 4.1 The principle that makes disjointness legible rather than arbitrary

> **Ordinary lines narrate WHAT HAPPENED. Decisive lines name WHAT IT MEANS.**

That is why the pools must not share members: they are doing different jobs, and a line that narrates
a corner cannot carry a moment that decides a bet. **It also explains the measured defect** — the
approach printed corner #1's line and the crossing printed corner #2's, so the two moments that
carried meaning were narrated by lines whose whole job was to narrate an event.

### 4.2 Four cells, not two — valence is read off the TICKET

The approach and the turn each split by the leg's side. **This is not new machinery:** `countHelps` is
already set from `leg.Selection.Choice` and `ScoreLedgerTests` already asserts that the mood follows
the bet, never the event.

| cell | when | line | authored short form |
|---|---|---|---|
| **APPROACH · OVER** | count reaches one short of the line | `one short. the ledger is holding its breath.` | `one short.` |
| **APPROACH · UNDER** | count reaches one short of the line | `one short, and the ledger would rather it stopped here.` | `one short.` |
| **TURN · OVER** | the count crosses — the leg is won | `that clears it. the line is beaten.` | `the line is beaten.` |
| **TURN · UNDER** | the count crosses — the leg is dead | `the line goes. the ledger closes this one.` | `the line goes.` |

**Register check:** lower-case, dry, mercantile — the deck's own voice (`another little number for the
ledger`, `Spreadsheets squeezing the half`). **No second person** — the existing deck never addresses
the player and these do not start.

**Both approach cells share a short form (`one short.`) and that is correct** — at the fallback rung
the fact is what survives, and the valence is already carried by the scene's own register.

### 4.3 One line per cell, and why that is enough

**A decisive beat fires at most once per leg**, so **repetition is unconstructible rather than
unlikely** — the property `T108` clause 1 asks for, arriving on copy instead of on a form.

The approach is a **state**, entered once when the count reaches one short and held (the corners watch
held it from 43' to 53'). **It fires on entry, not per frame and not per window.**

## 5. A CONSEQUENCE OF `T115` THAT BEARS ON ALL OF THE ABOVE

**A corner that earns no scene earns no strip line.** The scene and the strip take one grammar.

**But quiet must not mean STALE.** The measured defect was the strip holding a 65' line at 71',
narrating a count that had stopped mattering at 53'. **So the strip does not HOLD and does not go
BLANK — it falls to its calm register when the beat does**, using the existing neutral deck
(`SweatFlavor.NeutralLine`). **Nothing new is authored for the resting state; it takes the lines that
already exist.**

This is the same move as the grammar itself: **the resting state is not written, it is what remains.**
