# Room walkthrough — Allen's checklist (R22, gates 6–8)

**Why you:** the certification gates were signed off on geometry that has since
changed — the second bunk added a slab and posts. Automated checks can't judge
whether a space feels walkable and readable; a human walk is the named
instrument. ~10–15 minutes.

**Setup:** the room lead is staging the scene and will add exact open-and-move
steps below / in its note. Editor must be free (I coordinate the lease).

## What to check, in order

1. **The aisle under the new bunk slab.** The slab overhangs the aisle (~0.35m,
   at about head height, with posts in the lane). Walk it both directions:
   would a person duck, snag, or clip? Does it read as passable space?
2. **Doorway → bunk → desk → couch traversal.** Walk the full loop. Any spot
   where the path pinches unnaturally, any collider that stops you where the
   eye says you fit, any spot you pass through something solid.
3. **Seated sightlines.** Sit at the couch (TV view) and at the desk (laptop
   view): is the TV fully readable from the couch, the laptop from the chair?
   Does the second bunk block or crowd either view?
4. **Standing sightlines.** Standing at the door and at the window: does the
   room read as one coherent space — nothing floating, nothing intersecting,
   scale believable (objects are now true-world-size — beds read as beds,
   desk as a desk)?
5. **The phone.** It now renders the live bookie feed (your ruling): from
   normal positions, does it read as a working device and not a glowing
   rectangle?

## Verdict form (tell me in chat, plain words)

- Per item: fine / feels wrong (say where you were standing and what looked wrong).
- Overall: pass, or list of things to fix.

I turn your answers into the gate records; you don't need to touch any docs.
