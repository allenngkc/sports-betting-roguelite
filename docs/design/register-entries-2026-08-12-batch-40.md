## T85-am — the .02 em withdrawal's reason is NARROWED; .16 em and .10 em STAND

TV flagged `Label` .16 em and `Meta` .10 em as resting on the same kit-not-owning-document authority
as the withdrawn .02 em, and **did not act.** That is the correct call and it is endorsed: a lead
flagging an authority gap rather than resolving it by analogy is the contract working.

**The analogy does not transfer, and the reason is the whole of T85:** .02 em was withdrawn because it
was a **fourth variable inside an open verification pair** (C43-am(3)) — being unruled was the second
half of that finding, not the whole of it. **.16 em and .10 em did not join Phase T's variable.** They
predate it, they ship, and they sit in frames the studio has been reading for weeks.

**Ruled: they stand until the clause rules them.** Removing them now would be an unruled change to the
surface, in the opposite direction, on no evidence — the same defect the withdrawal corrected, with
the sign flipped. **An authority gap is not a licence to move a value; it is a reason to rule it.**

**The clause's scope is widened before it is written (C18 §4.1).** The owed tracking clause does not
cover "these three". **It inventories every slot on the surface carrying a tracking value, names each
one, and states the value and its basis** — because the three that surfaced are the three somebody
happened to look at, and the same kit-not-owning-doc basis plausibly covers more. T75 is the
precedent: twelve slots rendered by defaulting and the finding above the item was that half the
surface's type had no owner.

Written at this seat, against frames, after T84 clears. Until it exists none of the three values is
canon, and **none of them becomes canon by having shipped.**

cash-out slot, no actionable offer beside it.* So **the span is available by prior ruling**, which
makes span the first route here rather than a request. Order is unchanged: span, then size, **never

## T82 — the derived font landed; source verified, and what closes it

`tnum` resolved at build time, **every digit exactly 1000 units, spread 0, cmap-only change.** That is
the atlas route built exactly as granted — the font's own tabular glyphs, no outline invented, and a
cmap-only diff is the narrowest possible expression of it. The unit is right: the change lands on the
**asset**, which is where T75-am put the property.

**§2.5 holds the close open by one line.** *A source string is not a rendered element* — "every digit
1000 units, spread 0" is a measurement of the file. It is **necessary and not sufficient**, and this
is the same clause that made T82 a ruling rather than a note in the first place.

**What closes it, and the instrument already exists:** the rendered line from T75-am3 — within a set of
equal-character-count clock strings in the right-anchored slot, **the left ink edge is invariant**.
That is the acceptance test this seat re-pointed the batch-34 harness instrument at, and the after-set
already proved the instrument works by measuring the *proportional* case at 22.0 px spread with a
1.04 px residual. The tabular case is the same measurement expecting ~0.

**If TV's report carries that line, T82 CLOSES on this evidence.** If it does not, one line is owed and
nothing else.

**Still owed regardless, and unchanged from T82's grant:** the asset inventory naming its members —
every generated asset any figure slot renders on, confirmed tabular, **including the condensed Bold
700 asset that carries `CashOut`.** A cmap-only change is per-file; three assets need three
confirmations, and an inventory naming two of three is the defect C18 §4.1 exists for.

## G1-am4 — the tabular font is a NEW geometry event, and it lands on the row G1-am3 left provisional

G1-am3 recorded the compact column's **pass** as provisional against exactly this, and it has now
happened: **T82's font changes what a digit measures.**

`UNDER 10.5 CORNERS` and `UNDER 10.5 CNRS` passed 143 px **with room to spare against proportional
digits** — and tabular figures are typically wider than the proportional `1` they replace, because a
tabular set is normalised toward the widest digit. **That room is precisely what just changed.**

**Ruled: the compact column's digit-bearing rows RE-CERTIFY against the tree carrying the derived
font.** Same clause as every other geometry event on this certification (C18 §4.1), and this is the
fourth. The digit-free rows are final and are not re-read.

**The NEED column's failure is untouched** — every miss there is digit-free, which is exactly why
G1-am3 recorded it as final. The tabular font cannot rescue it and cannot worsen it.

**Consequence for the sweep, stated because it is running now:** if T84's sweep runs on a tree that
already carries the derived font, it catches this for free. If it ran before the font landed, **its
digit-bearing rows are void and re-run** — not wrong, just certifying a geometry that no longer
exists.
