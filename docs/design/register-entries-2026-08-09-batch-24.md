## S71 — the gate covers the ink. My stated gap is CLOSED at the token

Batch 22 asked what the gate asserts and what it cannot see, because I closed S71 with the ink read
qualitatively and not measured — and a string-only gate would have institutionalised that gap behind
something green, **created by my close rather than caught by it.**

**It is not string-only.** Assertions 4 and 5 pin both inks as `Color32` in the space they are authored
in. The vacuous-green shape did not happen.

**Assertion 5 is the one that matters, and the lane's reasoning for it is the ruling's own.** S71 held
that ownership does not need saying *because the header says it*. A gate reading only the empty line
holds half of that: a line that correctly stopped restating ownership, beside a header that quietly
stopped asserting it, leaves the column with no owner **and passes**. Reading both is what makes it the
ruling's gate instead of the string's — and the lane wrote it that way unprompted.

## C33-am4 — the per-element blindness is not only about rankings

**Amended** · DD 2026-08-09, second instance. C33(b) says *a per-element value check cannot see a
ranking*; C33-am2 says dominance and legibility are different instruments. Both were written about the
ladder, and **S71's gate is not a ranking** — it is an ownership relation between a header and a line.

**Widened: a per-element check cannot see any claim about a RELATIONSHIP between elements.** Rankings
are one kind. Ownership, voice, attribution and adjacency are others. **Where a ruling's truth depends
on two elements, both are gated, or the gate holds half a ruling.**

Founding second instance: this gate, which got it right by thinking rather than by rule — which is
exactly the case for writing the rule down. The next lane may reach for the element the ruling *names*
(the empty line) rather than the element that makes the ruling *true* (the header).

**Register-tier, not proposed for the constitution.** It amends an existing law rather than adding one,
and C33 is already carrying three amendments there.

## The four proposals — all four ruled, three granted as offered

**(d) — the substring guard. GRANTED, widened by one step.** `YOU HAVE NO MARKS` and `NO MARKS FOR
YOU` passing a `YOUR` guard is most of that assertion's value gone, and the lane said so. Guard the
**second-person pronoun class** — `YOU`, `YOUR`, `YOURS`, `YOU'RE` — as standalone words, not the one
substring.

Recorded so it is not mistaken for the ruling: **this is a proxy.** S71 forbids a second *speaker*;
second person is its most common symptom, not its definition. A pronoun guard is the practical
instrument and it is not the law.

**(c) — the assertion that does no work. GRANTED as the strengthening, not the deletion.** The lane's
own framing is right: assertion 6 cannot fail unless 4 or 5 already has, so **the gate looks like three
ink checks and is two.** Replace it with *the empty line's ink is not any of the player-ink tokens* —
independent of both, and the claim actually meant.

That strengthening carries C18 §4.1 with it by construction: *not any of the player-ink tokens*
requires the player-ink tokens to be **enumerated**, so the check names its members instead of counting
them. **The lane found its own miscount inside a gate it wrote, in a submission nobody asked for.**
Recorded as the standard.

**(e) — a sweep, not a gate. AGREED, and opened as S72.**

**(a) — the pixel close. REFUSED, and the reason is not cost.**

The residue is real: `TMP_Text.color` is what the build sets, not what the frame shows, and material,
SDF shader, grade, bloom and a parent `CanvasGroup` alpha all sit downstream. **But that residue is
surface-wide, not S71's**, and the surface already gates it:

- **The token gate catches ink-ABSENT** — the header silently ceasing to be biro. Nothing else does.
- **L3 catches ink-MISUSED on a rendered frame** — biro, wax or stamp appearing outside its meaning,
  including markup.
- **My close read the composed pair on Allen's frame**, qualitatively and correctly.

Those three cover both directions at three layers. What remains is *"does this element's authored token
survive the rendering path"* — a **question about the path, asked at one element**. Answering it per
item means every item on the surface eventually demands its own capture measurement, which is how a
studio spends a fortnight of capture windows proving what one surface-wide instrument already holds.

**S71 stays closed at the token, and the close stands.** If the rendering path is ever suspected of
moving authored inks, that is a surface-wide investigation with C38's shape — enumerate the path, not
the elements on it (C39).

## S72 — Empty-state voice inventory

**NEW — inventory only, bounded** · DD 2026-08-09, from the gate's blind spot (e).

S71 is a **voice** ruling and voice is surface-wide, but its gate covers one slot on one screen in one
state — FORM's `WorkingMargin` at zero picks. Nothing would catch a second speaker appearing in a
different empty state on a different screen.

**Scope, deliberately tight:**
- **Inventory the laptop's empty states** — every screen and slot that renders a "nothing here yet"
  statement: MY BETS, REWARDS, LEDGER with no settled records, old slips, the staged receipt, and
  whatever else the sweep finds. **Name the members** (C18 §4.1); a count is not an inventory.
- **For each: who speaks?** The state named, or the owner addressed.
- **No fixes inside**, no gate written, no rulings taken. Findings return here.
- **If the inventory comes back clean, S72 closes on that.** A clean sweep is a result.

**Justification stated honestly:** S67 earned its sweep on four instances over six days; this has
**one**. It is opened anyway because the cost is an inventory and the class is known-dangerous — this
studio has repeatedly found rulings that reached only the sites they named (S64, S67, T70's own
vertical duplication). **A one-instance sweep is a judgement call and it is recorded as one.**
