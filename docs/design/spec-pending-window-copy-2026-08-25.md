## 1. THE MEASURING STICK IS A STATE LIE, AND THAT DECIDES THE FORM

TV's placeholder is `DULUTH AUDITORS +1.5 IS DEAD`. **The leg is not dead.** The window exists
precisely because the loss is PENDING and the player may still spend to prevent it — that is the
whole affordance `§7c` preserves.

**The shipped decline row already proves the tense:** `N LET IT DIE`. Permissive, forward-looking —
it asks whether to allow something that has not happened. A row reading `IS DEAD` beside an option
reading `LET IT DIE` contradicts itself **on one row**.

So no authored form may assert the death. `T43`'s class, and the one place on this surface where
exactness is the entire point.

## 2. THE ONE-LEG FORM — SHIPS NOW

The decline row **absorbs the naming instead of sharing it**:

```
N LET AUDITORS +1.5 DIE
```

- **`T143` is satisfied** — the dead leg is named.
- **Nothing is restated.** `LET … DIE` supplies the verb and the tense; the name supplies its
  referent. The placeholder needed `IS DEAD` only because the name sat in a separate element.
- **This is option 3 at its tightest** — batch 186 ruled the copy shares the decline row; here it
  *is* the decline row, with no separator and no second element. Strictly shorter than the 528.4px
  shared form TV measured.
- **`T88(c)` survives**: still a press, still `N`-prefixed, still no `HOLD`. Batch 56's correction
  was about HOLD-versus-press, not about content.
- **`S24` survives**: one option, one row.
- Club token through `SweatFlavor.Short` (`T168-am`); handicap form `{CLUB} ±1.5` (`G1-am11` rung 3).

**The two spending rows are UNCHANGED** — `HOLD M MULLIGAN (ONE MULLIGAN SLIP)` and
`HOLD R SEND TO REVIEW (ONE REF'S WHISTLE)`. Three rows, 82.5px, exactly the shipped worst case.

## 3. THE ≥2 FORM — N-LIVE ONLY, LANDS WITH THE SHAPE THAT TRIGGERS IT

At two or more dead at one whistle, `T143-am2` removes the offer, so the spending rows do not
render. Batch 186's ruled composition, worded:

```
AUDITORS +1.5
SPREADSHEETS UNDER 2.5
NO ONE CALL SAVES THIS   ·   N LET THEM DIE
```

- **Bare names on their own rows.** With no option beside them there is nothing to contradict, and
  a list of two names under a frozen shot needs no verb — the third row supplies it for both.
- **`NO ONE CALL SAVES THIS`** is `S85`'s `NoSingleCallSaves`, stated BEFORE the offer would be —
  and it is why there is no offer, which is the refusal `S85` requires be shown rather than
  discovered.
- **`LET THEM DIE`**, plural, because the referent is now the list above it.
- Separator is the surface's own `   ·   ` (three spaces, middot, three spaces), the form
  `PreviewOf` already uses. `FitToColumn` drops a dangling ` ·` on truncation, so the shared row
  degrades cleanly rather than to a stranded glyph.

## 4. THE HOLE AT THREE — NAMED, NOT PAPERED OVER

Batch 186's arithmetic covers exactly two dead legs. **At three the composition is four rows and
the zone holds three.** Arm A makes it reachable — one fixture can carry three legs and resolve
them at one whistle (a same-match treble).

**No form is authored here for that case, and a count form would not rescue it:** `T143` requires
every dead leg be NAMED, and `THREE LEGS GO` names none. So this is a genuine conflict between a
ruling and a fixed zone, not a copy gap — **the same wall options 1 and 2 hit**, arriving from the
other side.

**Routed, not invented.** It needs either a `T143` amendment bounding the naming requirement, or
the zone question re-opened. This seat will not settle it inside a copy spec.
