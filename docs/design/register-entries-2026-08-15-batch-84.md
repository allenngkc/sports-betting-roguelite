## S85 — THE DEAD CLICK AT MaxLegs. A defect, and the rule it needs is general.

**Reported by the lane, which named it and invented nothing — the right behaviour, and it is the
gesture's own shipping cost surfacing rather than a regression.** A fifth pick at `MaxLegs` calls

### It is a defect, and by this surface's own first principle

**§8: the theatre prints facts and OFFERS.** **A price cell is an offer — the house stands behind it
and the player transacts against it.** **An offer that cannot be taken is not an offer**, and a
control that accepts a click and does nothing is the surface stating something it will not honour.
**Nothing is mispriced and nothing is hidden — but the surface has told him he may act, and then
not acted.**

### RULED — the general rule, because this is the first of a class the gesture will produce more of

> **Where a refusal is knowable BEFORE the act, the surface shows it before and the act never
> happens. Where it is knowable only AFTER, the stamp states cause and remedy (S73-am4, S77). A dead
> click is what happens when a knowable refusal is left to be discovered.**

**The two halves are already built and this only assigns them.** *These three cannot all win* is
unknowable until the combination exists — **that is the stamp's proper case.** *You already hold
four* is knowable the instant the fourth is marked — **so it belongs to the control, not to a
statement after the fact.**

### One trap named, because the obvious implementation walks into it

**The price cell already HAS a disabled treatment** — `frozen` dims it to `Dim(Muted)` and drops its
handler — **and reusing that exact treatment for MaxLegs would give two different causes one
appearance.** **`BOARD CLOSED` and `your slip is full` are not the same fact, and S2 bars a status
carried where it cannot be distinguished.** **Whatever is built must tell them apart.**
