## T74-am2 — the magnitudes, their levers, and the exemption that will be proposed and is unavailable

### RiskPays — 16.1 px over today, 39.8 px under tabular metrics

**Somebody will propose exempting RiskPays from tabular, and the argument is better than it looks.**
The mandate's stated mechanism is *"scores, clocks, money and counts all change in place; non-tabular
figures make the whole surface twitch on every tick"* — and **RISK and PAYS do not tick.** Both are
fixed the moment the ticket is placed; the cash-out figure is the one that moves. On the mandate's own
reasoning, and under C40's distinction between a rule's proxy (*"money"*) and the property it protects
(*changes in place*), a static money figure has no twitch to prevent. Nor does it gain the second
benefit of tabular figures — column alignment — because RISK and PAYS sit side by side, not stacked.

**The exemption is nonetheless refused, and not on that reasoning — it is unavailable by
construction.** T75-am settled the unit: the tabular property lives on the **font asset**, and a static
TMP asset bakes one set. T82 confirmed it: the fix is cmap-only, per asset. **`RiskPays` and `CashOut`
are both condensed Bold 700 — the same asset — and the cash-out figure ticks.** So exempting RiskPays
would require a *second condensed Bold asset for one slot*, which is the shape this studio has refused
three times (S70's fourth ground for one string, T77's second family for one slot, R41's second model).

**Ruled: RiskPays takes tabular because its asset-mate needs it.** The reasoning above is recorded
because it is sound and will recur — and because the next person to raise it deserves the answer that
it is right about the mechanism and defeated by the asset, not waved away.

**Lever: span, then size — never the copy** (§4 / T24-am). The value is T74's, on its own frames. The
constraint this seat sets: **the ticket column's outer width does not move** (T46 — the column owns
its width absolutely; R30 — a locked band is not headroom). Relief comes from inside the row.

### LegRowLine0 — passes by 3.4 px today, FAILS by 1.8 px on wiring

**This is the one that gates the wiring commit**, and it is the smallest number in the docket.

**The dead-backstop box and the wiring casualty are the same box.** With the backstop dead, an overrun
here would have gone out silently. TV fixed the backstop and re-measured through the live measurer —
which is the right direction, and it converts a silent defect into a loud one.

**But a working backstop is a DETECTOR, not a REMEDY, and the distinction is C40's.** *Truncation
fires* is the proxy; *the string fits* is the property. G1's entire charter is that **truncation is
never reached** — so a LegRowLine0 that truncates on the wiring commit is a violation that announces
itself, not a violation that has been handled. Nobody may read "the backstop works now" as closure.

**Ruled: 1.8 px of relief lands BEFORE wiring**, by span or size. See T82-d.

## T85-am2 — the tracking clause, ruled; `Label` .16 em and `Meta` .10 em RATIFIED; NEED stays 0

### The doctrine, and it is not new — it is the laptop's, applied

`surething-design.md` §4.3 already rules this one surface over, and S68 is the worked case: **short
uppercase labels are tracked; factual copy stays literal; a label-plus-instruction is factual copy.**
S70 added the shape for exceptions — OS chrome takes its own token, and *a named exception with one
member is still named*.

**That doctrine is a voice principle, not a surface-specific one, and it transfers: tracking marks a
LABEL. It never marks a FACT.** A tracked label says *this is furniture, read past it*; tracking a
fact spaces out the thing the player is supposed to read.

### The three known values, ruled

- **`Label` .16 em — RATIFIED as built.** A label-class slot, tracked as the doctrine wants.
- **`Meta` .10 em — RATIFIED as built.** Same class, lighter value for a smaller, quieter slot.
- **`NEED` .02 em — stays at 0, now on DOCTRINE and not only on procedure.**

**That last one is the substantive half.** T85 withdrew .02 em because it was a fourth variable inside
an open pair (C43-am(3)) — a *procedural* finding, which would have let the value return once the pair
closed. It does not return: **NEED is the requirement statement, the single most factual string on the
surface — it is what has to happen for the player's money to land.** Tracking it is tracking a fact.
The value that broke the procedure was also the only one of the three that breaks the doctrine, and
that convergence is why this can be ruled now rather than waiting on the inventory.

Both ratifications rest on frames the studio has been reading for weeks — **C23's shape**: the build's
value is the one that has been seen at review distance; the document's value has never existed.

### The clause lands in the owning document with its inventory OPEN and saying so

Written into `tv-design.md` §4. It carries the doctrine, the three ruled members, and **an explicit
statement that the inventory is incomplete pending the six unswept slots.**

**An honestly-bounded clause beats no clause**, and the constitution says so directly (§1.1 — a stub
is a legitimate state; an empty section pretending to authority is the failure). **The doctrine binds
now**, which is the point: without it, the six slots still being enumerated can acquire unruled
tracking while everyone waits for a complete list.

**Until a slot is named in that clause, its tracking value is not canon and does not become canon by
having shipped** — unchanged from T85-am.

## T82-d — the wiring word: NOT YET, on two conditions, both small and both running

Wiring is granted (T82-c) and the word is **held**, on exactly two conditions:

1. **LegRowLine0's 1.8 px lands first.** Wiring is otherwise a commit that *creates* a truncation on
   the leg statement — authorised by this seat, on a box the sweep told us about in advance. That is
   the one outcome the whole sweep existed to prevent.
2. **The six-slot enumeration completes.** An incomplete inventory cannot certify that LegRowLine0 is
   the *only* wiring casualty, and a second one discovered after the commit is C18 §4.1 collecting on
   a debt this seat chose to run.

**The principle behind the split, stated so it generalises: wiring is gated on the failures it
CREATES, not on the failures that already exist.** RiskPays is 16.1 px over *today* — that is T84's
blocker, it already holds the ship, and it does not additionally hold the wiring. LegRowLine0 passes
today and fails on the commit, so it does.

**On the word:** send the enumeration result and LegRowLine0's relief together and the wiring word
follows immediately — it needs no further ruling, only those two facts. If the enumeration turns up
further wiring casualties, the same test applies to each, and none of them needs a fresh docket.
