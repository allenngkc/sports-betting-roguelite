## 0. THE ONE MISTAKE THAT MUST NOT REPEAT

**The last forced set was shot on the wrong screen state.** `T133-am2`: the frames were the ticket
card at `PRE`, where `_ticket.State` is `Open` — **and the settled footer renders ONLY when the state
is `CashedOut` or `Lost`.** The branch never ran; the string was forced onto a composition the code
path cannot produce.

**`E2` is a SETTLED ticket. So is any frame carrying `RETURNED` or `PAID`.** If the clock reads `PRE`
or the stage shows a ticket card, the frame is not of this subject.

## 1. BINDING CONDITIONS

1. **`E2` discloses its forcing in every filename** (`S3`, `T133`'s set) — the enumerated fact floor
   cannot be dealt for.
2. **Every frame carries the CHROME ROW** (`R1/8 · BANK · PAY $60 · COMPS · SEED`). `T133-am2`'s live
   objection is that `PAID` collides at the root with `PAY $60`, and **that read is impossible unless
   both are in one frame.**
3. **`E3` carries a LIVE leg row and the footer TOGETHER.** A frame of the footer alone does not show
   this change — the height came from the leg rows and the cost lands there.
4. **`E1` carries the alignment arm** (`T147-am2`): left/left as built, plus the opposite-anchor arm,
   on one ruler.
5. **Read the alignment on the SETTLED state.** `RISK`/`PAYS` are both four characters and align
   either way; `STAKE`/`RETURNED` are five and eight, and that is where left/left can go ragged.

## 2. `E1` — the footer at ordinary values

**`T74-am6`'s own case: `$1,234` staked paying `$12,340`** — the plain 10× parlay it called *"not a
tail case."*

- **BINARY — the collision is gone.** Two rows, no overprint, at the values that produced 270.6px in
  a 249.0px row.
- **BINARY — each row fits its own full width**, which is what separate rows buys.
- **DIRECTION — the pair must still read as ONE statement.** What he put in, what comes back.
  **This is the real risk of separate rows and `T74-am5` chose one row partly for it**: two rows can
  read as two unrelated facts. If they do, the composition is right and its typography is not, and
  that returns here.
- **ALIGNMENT — not pre-committed.** `T147-am2` struck my supporting citation and the money control
  is a counter-precedent. **I hold no lean and will read the arms as shot.**

## 3. `E2` — the enumerated fact floor, forced

## 4. `E3` — the leg rows, where the cost landed

**The one to hold the phase on.**

- **BINARY — the live row still FITS.** Compact line, NEED and progress, at the new pitch, with
  `T24`'s 8.0px margin intact. **The gate measured a ceiling of 55.4px against 60.0 needed and
  `T147-am` paid for it by dropping two never-fillable slots — this frame is where that arithmetic
  meets a rendered row.**
- **DIRECTION — does the column read SPARSE?** Four rows at ~99px pitch for 58.8px of live ink,
  against today's 69.3px pitch with a 138.6px dark tail below the last leg.
- **MY LEAN, ON THE RECORD AND NOT BINDING: I expect it to read LOOSER BUT ACCEPTABLE, and I expect
  the loss of the dark tail to be a visible improvement** — a column that ends where its content ends.
  **This seat's leans have been overturned by frames more often than confirmed, which is why it is
  written down.**
- **THE THING I DO NOT KNOW AND WILL LOOK AT FIRST: whether a leg row's content is TOP-ANCHORED or
  CENTRED in its rect.** Top-anchored, the extra 40px pools beneath each row and reads as loose
  leading; centred, it reads as even spacing. **Same arithmetic, different result, and nothing in the
  ruling says which.**
- **IF IT READS SPARSE the next rung is already named** (`T147-am`): a fixed pitch with the remainder
  dark below the last leg, then money type 24 → 22, **which this seat reserves rather than spends.**
