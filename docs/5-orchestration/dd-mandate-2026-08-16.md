## Phase 1 — Market surfaces

The engine sells ~80 offers per matchup (draws, totals, corners, cards,
handicaps, correct scores, team totals, odd/even…) and the laptop board has
never had a deliberate presentation pass — the market sheets grew by
accretion.

**Allen's explicit first step: spawn a research agent to study real
sportsbook UIs — FanDuel, bet365, and peers** — for how they organize dozens
of markets per event: market grouping and naming, sheet navigation, row
anatomy, information density, how a long tail of markets stays browsable.
Bring back what transfers to our kit and what does not (we are a stylized
diegetic laptop, not a web app — the kit's constraints stand).

Then explore and spec how OUR board and ENTRY sheets present the full
vocabulary. Material choices go to Allen per the design flow. The
markets-pregame lane revives to build against your approved spec.

## Phase 2 — Theater for count markets
