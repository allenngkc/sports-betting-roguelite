# Phase 4 — conditional cash-out on a SAME MATCH ticket (scope)

**Lane:** 2 · **Plan:** F_0.6.0 · **2026-08-14** · **Status:** scoping doc, nothing built
**Canon:** `design/02-betting-math.md` § *Cash-out pricing* → *Same-game cash-out (2026-08-12)*
**Blocker cleared:** this was deferred until after step 4's gates; those are done.

---

## What is wrong today, precisely

`SweatSession.RawCashOutFair()` prices the remaining legs as `Π(p_j × o_j)` using each leg's
**pre-match marginal** `TrueProb`, over a **product** of resolved leg odds. For a same-match ticket
that is wrong twice over: the surviving legs are correlated with each other, and the ticket's price is
a locked joint, not a product of legs.

It is not currently *reachable* damage — the gate campaign's coverage strategy holds same-match
tickets to settlement precisely because of this, which is the coverage gap Phase 4 closes. But it is
live product code the moment a player cashes out a same-match ticket.

## The finding that sets the real scope

**The sweat resolves one leg at a time, and drama paths are per leg.** `SweatSession` asserts
`paths.Count == ticket.Legs.Count` and walks them with a single `_currentLeg` cursor. Live probability
is not derived from revealed match state at all — it is `e.WinProbAfter`, **generated by the drama
layer** as a narrative bridge from the pre-match probability to an outcome already sampled at lock.

So a same-match ticket with two legs on one match today gets **two independent narrations of the same
match, played sequentially**, neither reflecting the other. That is the leg-scoped event stream TV PRD
§4.3.1a flagged, whose fix it assigned to "whoever builds step 1's dependency" — this lane.

**But the pricing bug and the presentation gap are separable, and that is the useful insight.**
Under sequential resolution only one leg is ever live, so conditioning the *pending* legs on the
*settled* legs' outcomes is not an approximation — it is exact. Simultaneous live legs are a
presentation problem, not a pricing-correctness one.

## Options

| | Shape | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Match-scoped sweat** | one event stream per match; all same-match legs share a cursor and move together | correct and desirable, but it is a cross-lane architectural change to `DramaGenerator` + `SweatSession` + the TV surface. **Not required for pricing correctness.** Separable — recommend it be scoped with `tv-sweat`, not folded in here |
| **B. Condition on settled legs** | price the unsettled legs' joint by restricting the enumeration to outcomes consistent with what has already settled | **recommended.** Exact under sequential resolution, modest, engine-local |
| **C. Exclude same-match from cash-out** | what theScore Bet does (D1 research) | cheapest and honest, but deletes a core mechanic for this instrument only. Rejected unless B proves unaffordable |
| **D. Formalise hold-to-settlement** | make the campaign's workaround the product rule | same as C in effect, worse in framing — a workaround promoted to a design |

## Recommended shape (B)

```
fair = payout × P(every unsettled leg wins | the settled legs' outcomes)
```

with `payout = stake × the ticket's locked price` (void-adjusted via the survivor-subset table
already built in step 3). The conditional is the same enumeration the joint model already runs, with
the settled legs' results added as constraints — no new machinery, no new sample space.

Sanity anchors from canon: nothing settled → `fair = P(win) × payout`; everything settled →
`fair = payout`.

**Both anchors needed correcting once built (2026-08-14).** The first holds to **1.5e-14 relative,
not to the bit** — a single-selection enumeration is *close* to `MatchModel`'s board price, not
bit-identical, and the code comment that claimed otherwise has been corrected (132 of 244 legs
disagree by up to ~70 ulp, worst on `PlayerMultiScorer`). The claim that *is* exact, and the one the
same-match path rests on, is `p_joint(all legs) == SameMatch.PTicket`, asserted with `==`.

The second anchor is **unreachable inside `SweatSession`**: the session completes and cash-out goes
unavailable before "everything settled" can be quoted. It survives at `OddsMath` level, already
pinned. The session-level form is `fair = payout × liveProb` with only the last leg left, walking to
the payout as that leg closes. Ordinary tickets must keep their **exact** current figure — the same
bit-identity discipline that governed step 3 and P2, enforced by keeping the existing path verbatim
rather than recomputing it.

### The one genuinely open question

The **in-progress** leg's `_liveProb` is a drama-generated number, while the pending legs' conditional
comes from enumeration — and on a same-match ticket those two are correlated, so multiplying them
re-introduces in miniature the independence error being removed.

Two candidate rules, and this needs ratification rather than a lead's quiet choice:

1. **Re-weight** — compute the remaining legs' joint conditioned on settled outcomes, then scale by
   the ratio of the live leg's drama probability to its own conditional marginal. Keeps the
   correlation structure and honours the drama number the player is watching.
2. **Condition only on settled legs**, treating the live leg's drift as presentation and leaving it
   out of the quote's correlation structure. Simpler, and visibly ignores a number on screen.

Recommend **1**. It is writable, it keeps the quote consistent with what the player sees ticking, and
its EV remains auditable — which is the standing law that decides whether a mechanic is designed yet.

## Cost and risks

- **Engine-local**, `SweatSession` + the joint model's conditioning entry point. No Unity, no lease.
- **The `SubEvens`/refund path and cash-out interact** and neither has met the other: a voided
  same-match ticket already re-prices, and cash-out must read the same survivor-subset price rather
  than re-deriving one.
- **Free Bet's loss-side refund** is added to the quote today; under a joint it must use the
  conditional, not `Π p`.
- **Gate re-run required.** Closing the coverage gap means the campaign's strategy stops holding to
  settlement, so G1–G7 must be re-validated on a fresh held-out namespace — `HOLDOUT6-`;
  `HOLDOUT`…`HOLDOUT5` are burned.
- **Cash-out margin interacts with `κ`** and neither has been tuned against the other.

## What this does not include

Option A (match-scoped sweat / simultaneous live legs) and `TicketState.Voided`'s render arm — both
standing follow-ups owned elsewhere.
