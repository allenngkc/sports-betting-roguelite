## Balatro

**B1 — Timing.** From clicking "Play Hand" to the score landing: roughly how many seconds? Does the
animation ever make you *wait* for a result you already know?
→ closes `02-autopsy-balatro.md` §1 UNREACHED. Establishes the genre's resolution baseline against SBR's
multi-leg phase.

**B2 — Where the loss lands.** When you lost a run, did it feel like *the score requirement* beat you, or
*a boss blind's rule* beat you?
→ tests my inference that the ante curve is a pacing device, not a pressure lever (§2). If it was the
requirement, that inference is wrong and it changes how we read our own payment curve.

**B3 — The ladder.** Which stake did you reach, and what made you stop climbing — difficulty, boredom, or
just moving on?
→ RF-5. The funnel says the ladder is the retention engine; your stopping point is one first-hand data point
on why people stop.

**B4 — Upside rescue.** Did you ever win a run you felt you had *lost*, rescued by a Joker or a shop offer?
→ RF-6. Whether the references' upside-variance rescue is actually felt, or only exists on paper.

**B5 — Run length, wall-clock.** How long was a typical run for you, honestly? And did that change as you
got better?
→ RF-4. Run length is the binding variable in the whole win-rate argument, and the "30–60 min" figure in
`01-core-loop.md` is a straw man nobody has checked.

**B6 — The stop moment.** When you ended a session, what was on screen? Just lost? Just won? Mid-shop?
→ session shape; no instrument I have can see this.

## CloverPit

**C1 — Where the tension is.** During the spin, or watching the debt counter between spins?
→ closes `03-autopsy-cloverpit.md` §1 and is the load-bearing evidence for **S3** (author tension in the
room, not only in the resolution). If you say "during the spin", S3 weakens considerably.

**C2 — The pity flip.** Did you ever notice the Luck schedule — the sense that mercy arrives on a timetable?
If so, roughly when in your time with it, and did noticing make it better or worse?
→ `design/09` records the player phrase *"no longer gambling — engineering fate"*, which is almost exactly
our own fantasy line. Whether that flip is discoverable in practice decides whether **the information-axis
pity in RF-6 / STEAL is worth designing.**

**C3 — Did you escape?** ("The Structure" — get out of the Room. 30.9% of owners have.) If yes, roughly how
many runs did it take? If no, what stopped you?
→ RF-4, directly. Runs-to-first-win is the number the whole win-rate argument turns on, and you are the only
first-hand source available.

**C4 — Discomfort.** Did the gambling theme ever make you uncomfortable, and did that hurt or help?
→ pillar 4 calibration. A third of CloverPit's reviewers discuss it explicitly as gambling, and its positive
rate sits 7.5 points below Balatro's. That trade is the one `00-vision` signs SBR up for.

**C5 — Timing.** Spin commit to payout resolved: roughly how many seconds?
→ closes §1 UNREACHED.

**C6 — The 666.** When the house's counterattack took a win off you, was that *good*? Did it make you spin
again faster, or put you off?
→ SBR has no house-counterattack lever. If it reads as good, it is a candidate; if it reads as cheating, our
bounded-p doctrine is further vindicated.
