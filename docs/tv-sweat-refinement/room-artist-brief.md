# Note to the room artist lead — TV light colour

Copy-paste below.

---

**Subject: TV sweat light colour — the TV is now a blue/magenta light source**

Quick heads-up on something that affects the room lighting rig.

We've locked the visual world for the TV sweat: a stadium-LED language. Practically, that means the
TV is no longer a neutral white glow — **it emits saturated electric blue and hot magenta**, and it
is the dominant light source in the room whenever a sweat is running.

What we need from the room side:

1. **The TV's light should visibly colour the room.** Blue and magenta spill onto the peeling walls,
   the bunk frame, the couch, and the wet floor. In our concept render this reads beautifully — the
   room stops being olive and becomes the reaction shot.
2. **The yellow-green fluorescent stays.** We want the clash. Sickly institutional yellow-green in
   one corner, saturated blue/magenta from the TV — two light sources that do not agree. The olive
   room palette is still correct for everything the TV isn't lighting.
3. **Question we need answered:** how far can the TV realistically push the room's colour in-engine?
   Our render has it overriding the entire space. If the lighting rig can support that, it's a
   mechanic we want to use — big events could push the room further. If it can only tint locally
   around the TV wall, tell us now, because we'd design the payoff moments differently.

No action needed beyond a read on point 3. Nothing about the room's geometry, props, or material
palette changes — this is purely about what colour of light the TV throws and how far it reaches.
