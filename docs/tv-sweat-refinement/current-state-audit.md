# TV Sweat Refinement — Current-State Audit

**Scope:** read-only design-gate audit of the owned TV/theater runtime and its EditMode/PlayMode tests.  No runtime session, seed, screenshot, or test run is claimed below.  Every finding is tagged **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED** (source/test inspection) or **HYPOTHESIS-NEEDS-RUNTIME** (a risk that static inspection cannot settle).

## 1. Scene vocabulary, resolution, and variants

- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — all 16 templates:** `GoalFor`/`GoalAgainst` (1/2), `BreakawayFor`/`BreakawayAgainst` (3/4), `TerritoryFor`/`TerritoryAgainst` (5/6), `NearMissHope`/`NearMissScare` (7/8), `CalmPossession` (11), `LegFinalWon`/`LegFinalLost` (12/13), `Kickoff` (14), `Fallback` (15), `CornerFor`/`CornerAgainst` (16/17), and `Booking` (18) are the complete enum in [ScenePlaybook.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/ScenePlaybook.cs#L14) (lines 14–32). #9 lead-change and #10 swing are overlays, not enum values (lines 6–12, 45–48).
- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — variants 0/1/2:** `VariantFor(step)` is deterministic `step mod 3`, including negative correction, in [ScenePlaybook.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/ScenePlaybook.cs#L88) (lines 88–95). The only common spatial difference is `Lane`: 0=center (.50), 1=.32, 2=.68 ([TheaterStage.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TheaterStage.cs#L762), lines 762–766); breakaway also uses variant 2 to choose the opposite shot y (.58 vs .42) (lines 836–846). There is no per-template authored movement grammar beyond those lane/shot substitutions.
- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — resolver order:** count markets take corner/booking only for non-finals and non-zero planned batches; final, near-miss, base type/direction, then overlays follow ([TheaterChoreographer.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TheaterChoreographer.cs#L47), lines 47–127). `Score` maps goal, `BigPlay` breakaway, `Momentum` territory/calm; reconciliation can upgrade a non-goal template to GoalFor/Against (lines 95–127).
- **HYPOTHESIS-NEEDS-RUNTIME — movement legibility:** Goal, breakaway, territory, near-miss, calm, corner, and booking have distinct step timing/routes in source ([TheaterStage.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TheaterStage.cs#L817), lines 817–945), but no test compares visible trajectories, carrier identity, formation silhouettes, or distinguishes all 0/1/2 variants at couch distance. The specified movement-design gate remains runtime work.
- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — truth risk:** a goal template can be full goal drama but `Commits=false`; `CompleteGoal` then does not move score ([SweatPresentationModel.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/SweatPresentationModel.cs#L297), lines 297–315), and TV copy becomes `VAR — NO GOAL` ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L735), lines 735–768). This is intentionally truthful at the ledger level, but static-only review cannot establish whether the unchanged visual read before VAR is sufficiently clear.

## 2. Determinism, presentation randomness, and engine isolation

- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — engine isolation:** choreography and both ledgers are pure presentation objects that bind to the locked stat line ([SweatPresentationModel.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/SweatPresentationModel.cs#L190), lines 190–222; lines 365–369). TV advances engine truth only through `MoveNext`, cash-out acceptance, and consumable paths; the theater is called afterward ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L521), lines 521–553). This is reinforced by the theater’s stated presentation-only RNG boundary ([TheaterStage.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TheaterStage.cs#L9), lines 9–20).
- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — deterministic presentation parts:** template variant is step-derived; team palette is name-hashed rather than engine-RNG derived ([SweatPresentationModel.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/SweatPresentationModel.cs#L532), lines 532–537); count batches are deterministically distributed and converge to endpoint ([SweatPresentationModel.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/SweatPresentationModel.cs#L457), lines 457–529).
- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — nondeterministic cosmetics:** `TheaterStage` seeds `System.Random` from `Environment.TickCount` plus a static salt ([TheaterStage.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TheaterStage.cs#L164), lines 164–175) for idle passes/noise; TV uses `UnityEngine.Random.value` for emission seed ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L390), lines 390–399) and `UnityEngine.Random.Range` for static texture ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L2132)); confetti has a fixed local `System.Random(0x534252)` and consumes it per celebration (lines 390, 1647–1663). These do not prove engine state mutation, but prevent reproducible pixel-identical playback.
- **HYPOTHESIS-NEEDS-RUNTIME — RNG side effects:** no inspected test snapshots engine RNG/cursor before and after theater/UI creation, and Unity global RNG is used by UI cosmetics. Engine isolation is architecturally intended but needs an integration assertion to prove no observable engine stream effect.

## 3. Goals, counts, near misses, and scorer identity

- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — goals and score convergence:** only `OnGoalPlayed` calls `CompleteGoal` and updates the scorebug ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L735), lines 735–768). Goals are staged at beat resolution but commit on playback completion; final planning emits remaining locked endpoint goals ([SweatPresentationModel.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/SweatPresentationModel.cs#L224), lines 224–353). The score tests cover staging, clamp, reconciliation, final matrix, and endpoint convergence ([ScoreLedgerTests.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/Tests/EditMode/ScoreLedgerTests.cs#L17), lines 17–333).
- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — near miss:** the resolver always gives NearMissHope/Scare no `Goal` ([TheaterChoreographer.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TheaterChoreographer.cs#L87), lines 87–93); stage uses one shared shot/save/bar/clearance script, mirrored for scare ([TheaterStage.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TheaterStage.cs#L892), lines 892–904). The EditMode test pins "never a goal" ([TheaterChoreographerTests.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/Tests/EditMode/TheaterChoreographerTests.cs#L54), lines 54–65), but does not distinguish block/interception/keeper-save/clearance/post/wide visuals.
- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — corners/bookings:** a count scene only occurs for a positive scheduled batch; corners fire at kick and bookings at card marker ([TheaterChoreographer.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TheaterChoreographer.cs#L51), lines 51–73; [TheaterStage.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TheaterStage.cs#L851), lines 851–879). `OnCountPlayed` is the count mutator ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L771), lines 771–787), and `CountLedger` guarantees monotone capped totals/final remainder planning ([SweatPresentationModel.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/SweatPresentationModel.cs#L469), lines 469–515). Static coverage includes schedule and endpoint tests ([ScoreLedgerTests.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/Tests/EditMode/ScoreLedgerTests.cs#L284), lines 284–318), not in-scene callback-to-visible-count integration.
- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — scorer behavior:** scorer-leg band reconciliation is suppressed ([SweatPresentationModel.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/SweatPresentationModel.cs#L218), lines 218–221); identity is withheld before final sequence and selected from locked home/away scorer lists only at final playback ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L1082), lines 1082–1093). The post-goal line says "THAT'S YOUR MAN" only if identity matches selected player (lines 758–767). Betslip identity coverage exists ([AnytimeScorerBetslipTests.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/Tests/EditMode/AnytimeScorerBetslipTests.cs#L8)), but no inspected PlayMode test exercises a scorer win and loss through TV stage/copy.
- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — scorer actor-binding gap (staff review):** identity is withheld until `_finalSequenceActive`, but `PrepareScoringActor` is called only in the non-final beat path ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L568), lines 568–597); neither final branch calls it (lines 643–672). Even when called, `SetScoringActor` only renames a roster-indexed dot ([TheaterStage.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TheaterStage.cs#L342), lines 342–349), while receivers and the shot route are chosen independently from nearest authored waypoints (lines 479–515). The named scorer is therefore not bound to the visible final touch. Runtime evidence is still required for a complete bug-ledger row.

## 4. Market, intervention, seating, and transition behavior

- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — cash-out suspend/reopen:** dangerous goal/count/near-miss scenes suspend the label and stop price animation; `RevealBeatChrome` reopens at payoff ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L615), lines 615–639; lines 1349–1408). Cash-out is rejected while suspended, while amount animation runs, unseated, complete, pre-first-event, or no offer ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L1755), lines 1755–1769). The UI can display a rounded/interpolated offer, but acceptance is explicitly blocked until its coroutine ends (lines 1428–1483).
- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — cash-out input-reservation gap (staff review):** `CashOutLive()` ignores both `_marketSuspended` and `_cashOutAnimation` ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L441), lines 441–443), even though `TryCashOut()` rejects those states. `SitSpot` uses that broader predicate to suppress standing ([SitSpot.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/SitSpot.cs#L83), lines 83–89). With an underlying offer, Interact can therefore be reserved while the visible state is suspended/updating, yet perform neither cash-out nor the normal stand action. Runtime capture and rate remain pending.
- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — Mulligan/Whistle pending path:** a pending final starts an opponent kill shot and holds it at the shot; input offers Mulligan, Ref’s Whistle, or decline while seated ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L643), lines 643–673; lines 832–895). Continuation is selected from resolved ticket-local `Won`/`Lost`/`Voided`, not `WinProbAfter` ([TheaterStage.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TheaterStage.cs#L365), lines 365–391). TheaterStage PlayMode tests cover lost and void continuation/frozen ball ([TheaterStageTests.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/Tests/PlayMode/TheaterStageTests.cs#L176), lines 176–215), but not the screen’s consumable input or Whistle-success path.
- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — core scene stand/resume:** every frame propagates seating to `_stage.SetFrozen(!_seated)` ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L1723), lines 1723–1753); the outer ticket/event holds use `SeatedHold` ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L521), lines 521–553; lines 1858–1873). Stage `Update` returns while frozen ([TheaterStage.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TheaterStage.cs#L425), lines 425–433).
- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — incomplete exact-frame pause (staff review):** `ScaledWait` and `WaitRealtime` always advance from `Time.deltaTime` ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L1876), lines 1876–1886). Cash-out interpolation, resolution effects, ticket-dead dimming, win tally/confetti, and other raw-time loops likewise do not consult `_seated` (for example lines 1451–1483 and 1557–1634). Standing freezes core scene motion and the event cursor, but does not implement the proposed literal pause across all visible ceremonies/transitions. Existing screen coverage asserts only the event cursor ([TvSweatScreenTests.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/Tests/PlayMode/TvSweatScreenTests.cs#L72)); the runtime phase must capture visible failures and reproduction rates.
- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — ticket/final/settle sequencing:** ticket card calls `ResetForNewSession` before it renders ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L509), lines 509–519); the sweat loops serially, and after last sweat calls `FinishAndSettle` then settle card ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L502), lines 502–506). Final slam resolves revealed leg, ceremony, then reopens market ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L691), lines 691–733). The two-ticket PlayMode smoke test asserts sessions and terminal ticket states ([TvSweatScreenTests.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/Tests/PlayMode/TvSweatScreenTests.cs#L43), lines 43–61), not zero stale score/tape/offer across transition.
- **HYPOTHESIS-NEEDS-RUNTIME — transition reliability:** there is no source-level test matrix proving cash-out reopen visibility, interaction timing, Mulligan/Whistle outcome permutations, all seating states, or stale-chrome absence over multiple tickets/final settle. These are the highest-value design-gate runtime checks.

- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — typography:** scorebug uses 22px leg/clock, 34px matchup, 20px records, and 15px slip strip; theater layout reflows flavor 22px, win pct 20px, cash-out 26px ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L1923), lines 1923–1965; lines 2020–2039). Text is overflow-enabled ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L2041), lines 2041–2054), so required-copy truncation/wrap is not guarded.
- **EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED — colors:** current fields are phosphor green `(0.20,1.15,.40)`, hot red `(1.10,.16,.13)`, gold `(1.15,.82,.18)`, cyan `(.62,.86,.96,.95)`, near-black background `(.015,.03,.022,.86)`, and bar `(.05,.08,.06,.92)` ([TvSweatScreen.cs](../../unity/SBR/Assets/SBR/Runtime/TvSweatScreen.cs#L287), lines 287–294). ## Design-gate conclusion

**EVIDENCE-CONFIRMED:** factual staging, callback ordering, presentation-ledger convergence, and high-level session sequencing have substantial static/unit coverage. Staff review also confirmed three code-path gaps to prioritize in Phase 1A: cash-out input reservation disagrees with suspended/updating acceptance, exact-frame pause is incomplete outside the core scene loop, and the named final scorer is not bound to the visible final touch. **HYPOTHESIS-NEEDS-RUNTIME:** complete bug rows still require observed behavior, exact seed/round/ticket/market context, reproduction rates, and visual evidence; source inspection cannot replace those runs.
